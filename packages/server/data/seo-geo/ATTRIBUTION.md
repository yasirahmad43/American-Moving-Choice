# SEO geo dataset: sources, licences and attribution

Generated by `packages/server/scripts/build-seo-geo.ts` (`pnpm --filter @amc/server build:seo-geo -- <source-dir>`). Do not edit by hand. Built on 2026-10-28.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `states.json` | The 50 states and DC: code, slug, name, centroid (mean of GeoNames ZIP centroids), ZIP count and county count. |
| `cities.json` | 29,639 places, one row per state + slug: `[stateCode, slug, name, county, countyFips or null, lat, lng, population or null, zips[]]`. County and coordinates come from the attached GeoNames ZIPs (majority county, mean centroid) when a place has ZIPs, otherwise from the city CSV. Population is known for 914 places (the top-1000 list); ZIPs are attached by place name. |

ZIP codes themselves come from `../zip5.json` (GeoNames, see `../README.md`), not from this folder.

## What was kept

- Only places in the 50 states and DC (99 rows outside were dropped).
- 142 duplicate rows (same state and place) were dropped: the first row wins. When two different places in one state share a slug, the second gets the county appended (`springfield-clark-county`, site architecture §2.2).
- Coordinates are rounded to four decimals. Population is the plotly figure as published (no year is given in that file, so pages describe it as "population (latest published figure)").

## Known limits

- Population covers only the 1,000 largest places; every other place has `null` and no page cites a population for it.
- A ZIP is attached to a city by name match only; ZIPs whose place name matches no city row stay unattached (the ZIP table still holds them with their GeoNames place name).
- Distances are straight-line (haversine) between centroids, not driving distances.
