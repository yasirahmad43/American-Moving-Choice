## 2. Purpose and reader

**Purpose.** Publish the review method that mover verification §6 ("Published Review Methodology page covering eligibility, verification labels, moderation, calculation and appeals") and site architecture §7.2 require. This page owns who can review, the evidence labels, invitations, moderation, ratings, minimum samples and AI summaries (content README §9). Every profile, review section and label chip links here (site architecture §3.2 Profile row; visual direction §9.4), so the page has to answer "can I trust this rating?" on its own.

**Readers.**
1. A person on a mover's profile deciding how much weight to give its rating and reviews. This is the main reader.
2. A person who got a review invitation and wants to know what happens to the review and the documents.
3. A mover checking what it can and can't do with reviews. The page covers only the consumer-facing facts. Mover detail belongs on `/for-movers/reviews-and-reputation/`.
4. Counsel, journalists and AI answer engines summarizing AMC's review practice. "American Moving Choice reviews" is one of the brand prompts in site architecture §7.7.

**Job.** Decide whether the reviews are real and fairly shown, know how to review and how to report a review, and see that payment doesn't buy ratings.

## 10. Open items

1. **Post-move invitation (owner).** This is the main decision (§8, first row). Adopting it changes mover verification §6 (Collection), JTBD §4 J1 stage 13 and J6 stages 1a and 1b, and backlog 6.5.1 and 6.5.2 (implications §4.4, §10). It adds an invitation and doesn't end company-requested ones, so the data model §2 role matrix ("request reviews" for owners and managers) and `review_invitations.requested_by` stay as they are unless the owner separately decides to end those (§8, second row). An earlier version of this item listed those data-model changes as consequences of the default; implications §4.4 and §10 don't support that.
2. **Which move date starts the post-move count.** The survey accepts a date or a flexible range (implications §2.1 step 3). Decide which date starts the `{{setting.post_move_invite_days}}` count when a range is given. The post-move [Owner] marker names it.
3. **Post-move invitations on shared requests.** Does every mover that bought a shared request trigger its own post-move invitation, or only one? The page says each invitation is about one mover, which implies one per buyer. The post-move [Owner] marker names it.
4. **What a review shows about its writer.** Implications §8.3 proposes first name and initial and the states involved. No rule is adopted, so the page is silent.
5. **Failed or missing evidence.** No document says what happens when an uploaded document fails the check. A move review whose label can't rate the moving experience is now a §8 row with a page marker. Also decide whether a completion document alone (a bill of lading, final invoice or delivery receipt, with no estimate, order or deposit receipt) can earn Verified completed move. Mover verification §6 requires the Verified customer document as well, and the page now says so.
