## 2. Purpose and reader

**Purpose.** Give a person planning a move a sourced, dated planning range before any contact, in words that can't be read as a quote, and an optional next step tied to real coverage. Site architecture §1.2 asks for "a server-rendered page with an H1 and a working range estimator that shows a labeled range before any contact field", one model version for the widget, the results view and the survey prefill, and wording that "must not read as a quote or binding estimate". MVP scope S6 and backlog 7.7 ask for stated assumptions, sources and date, with no contact details required.

**Readers.**
- **Primary:** a person early in planning who searched "moving cost calculator", "moving cost estimator" or "moving estimate" and wants "a realistic price expectation before talking to anyone" (JTBD §3.1). They may be moving locally or across states.
- **Secondary:** readers arriving from the cost hub, the cost spokes or a route page; answer engines reading the static sections; and engineers on slice S3 (build plan §11), who map the strings by ID.

**What the reader should leave with:** a range with its scope, source, date and version, or an honest reason there isn't one; what the range includes and leaves out; why a mover's written estimate after a survey, not a range, prices the move, with a link to How movers charge for the details; and a way to see which movers cover the move, with nothing sent until they choose.

- **"moving estimate".** Some searchers want an estimate from a mover. The page says what a planning range is, why a mover's written estimate after a survey prices the move, and offers the call to action. The copy says "not a quote or an estimate", which is wider than the map note's "binding estimate" and matches ST-6 and ST-7; "not a binding estimate" alone could imply AMC gives non-binding ones. - **"long distance moving cost calculator".** The meta description names long-distance moves, and the route summary and results name the move type and distance band.
- **Not targeted.** The "how much do movers cost" family belongs to `/moving-costs/` (one of ten shared top-10 URLs with the calculator; site architecture §3.1). The method page targets no keyword and avoids these terms (method brief §3).
