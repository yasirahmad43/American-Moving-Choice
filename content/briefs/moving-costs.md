## 2. Purpose and reader

**Purpose.** Answer "how much do movers cost?" honestly while American Moving Choice has no cost data. The page says what drives the price, how local and long-distance moves are charged, and how a reader gets to a real number: a mover's written estimate after a survey, with the calculator's planning range where data allows. It holds the gated ranges table by home size and move type, which publishes only when rate cards from enough movers support it (site architecture §6.3). As the pillar of the Moving costs cluster, it links the spokes, the calculator, the method page and How movers charge (site architecture §3.1, §3.2).

**Readers.**
- **Primary:** a person planning a household move who wants a realistic price expectation before talking to anyone, and who worries the price will change on moving day (JTBD §3.1: the functional job "Get a realistic price expectation before talking to anyone", the emotional job "Feel confident that the price I'm quoted won't double on moving day" and pain 2). Local and long-distance alike, at any home size; the 1-bedroom query (keyword map line 56) lands on the table's anchor.
- **Secondary:** readers arriving from the calculator, the method page, How movers charge or the planned spokes; answer engines and journalists that quote cost figures, which is why every row carries its scope, source, sample size and date (implications §6.8, §7.2). The page carries no B2B call to action (site architecture §1.1).

**What the reader should leave with:**
- price depends mainly on distance and move type, home size and services, with access, timing, valuation and extra charges on top;
- local moves are usually charged by the hour, long-distance moves mainly by weight and distance;
- typical planning ranges by home size and move type where the data allows, each scoped, sourced, sized and dated, and a plain "not yet" until then;
- the way to an accurate price: a written estimate after a survey, compared like for like, with the right questions;
- FMCSA's red flags that touch on price;
- American Moving Choice doesn't price moves; movers do.
