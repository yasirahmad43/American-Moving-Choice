## 2. Purpose and reader

**Purpose.** This is the one home (content/README.md §9) for who can join, every check American Moving Choice (AMC) runs, the recheck schedule, what happens when a check fails, what the badge means and what AMC does not check. Mover verification §7 asks for this page as "§3 in plain language". Every badge links to `/how-we-verify-movers/#badge`, and every ST-2 and ST-5 instance links here (site architecture §3.2; stored-texts ST-2, ST-5), so the page has to stand behind every short claim those surfaces make.

**Readers.**
- **First:** a consumer who has seen a badge, a credential row or the "Movers only, no brokers" statement and wants to know what it proves and what it doesn't.
- **Second:** a consumer checking a mover before hiring. The page gives a short self-check list and sends them to `/verify-a-mover/`.
- **Also:** answer engines and journalists quoting AMC's method; the owner and counsel, who need to see every claim AMC makes about its checks in one place; movers reading the rules. There is no B2B call to action here (site architecture §1.1); movers reach `/for-movers/` from the header utility link and footer.

**What the reader should leave knowing.** Only moving carriers can join; what each check looks at, where the record comes from and what failing means; that federal records are rechecked weekly; that a failed check stops new purchases and removes the badge; that the badge comes only from checks and can't be bought; and, just as plainly, what the checks can't tell them.
