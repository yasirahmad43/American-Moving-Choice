# How feedback works on American Moving Choice

After dealing with a mover through an American Moving Choice request, you may receive a single-use invitation to share feedback about that experience. Feedback helps our team review conduct and service issues. We don't display public company profiles, company review pages, preferred-mover showcases or ranked mover lists.

## Invitation-only feedback

Only a consumer whose request a mover purchased can use the invitation issued for that interaction. Each invitation has its own single-use link. There is no open review form where anyone can submit a rating for any company.

The invitation identifies the company it concerns. This feedback step follows the request and purchase process; it isn't a way to browse companies or request quotes from a particular mover before contact.

## Two different experiences

We keep feedback about the quote and sales experience separate from feedback about the move itself. A company purchasing a request doesn't show that you hired it or that a move happened.

| Evidence label | What it records |
|---|---|
| Verified platform inquiry | Our records connect the request to the company that purchased it. |
| Verified customer | Supporting documents show a customer relationship beyond the request. |
| Verified completed move | Supporting evidence shows that the move was completed. |

These labels describe the evidence available for a feedback record. They aren't a government certification, a recommendation, or a guarantee of a company's work. Marking a lead completed in a company's own pipeline isn't proof on its own.

## How we handle submissions

Feedback goes through moderation. We review its relationship to the request and check for unrelated content, conflicts of interest, threats, abuse or private personal information. A low rating or negative account is not, on its own, a reason to reject feedback.

Movers can't edit or delete a consumer's submission. A company may respond or raise a concern through its account, and our team records moderation decisions. Moderation follows the same standards for every submission.

Supporting documents and contact details aren't presented in public company listings. Avoid including other people's private information in your account of what happened.

## Request history and feedback

Feedback invitations relate to a specific consumer-company interaction recorded for a request. A larger number of invitations isn't evidence of better service.

We don't use feedback to publish a list of “best” or “preferred” movers. [How we verify movers](/how-we-verify-movers/) describes the separate screening process.

## Reporting a problem

You don't need to wait for a feedback invitation to tell us about a problem. Use [Report a concern](/report-a-concern/) if a company contacts you outside your permission, doesn't identify itself, shows credentials that look wrong, acts as a broker, or behaves badly. Include your request reference and the company name it gave during contact, if known.

For loss, damage or a dispute about a mover's bill, see [Complaints](/complaints/). Feedback doesn't replace a claim with the mover or a report to the relevant agency.
