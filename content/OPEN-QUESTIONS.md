## The five most consequential questions

1. **C2. Do AMC's role statements, and its paid delivery of requests to Florida movers, make it a broker under Florida ch. 507 or under federal "holding out" rules?** Florida §507.01(10) (2025 text) counts anyone who "for compensation, refers a shipper to a registered mover" as a moving broker. If ch. 507 reaches AMC for moves within Florida, it brings registration with FDACS, a $50,000 bond, broker advertising rules and a ban on brokers giving estimates. That ban would reach the calculator and any Florida page. Separately, ST-1a "We are not … a moving broker", ST-1c and ST-1e state legal conclusions, and 49 U.S.C. 13102(2) and 49 CFR 371.103 reach anyone who holds itself out as arranging transport. The current text of §507.02 (scope) and the 2026 statute have not been seen (V5). *Default in the drafts:* none on the law. The factual variant "We hold no FMCSA operating authority, as a mover or as a broker…" is offered in the ST-1 notes, and ST-1d already uses it. *Blocks:* 8 pages (How AMC makes money, Movers vs brokers, How movers charge, Moving costs, Which companies and Calculator through ST-1a/1b/1e; Calculator method and Calculator through the estimate ban; How we verify through Florida credentials) and every state hub.
2. **C1. Do planning ranges, the ranges table and the calculator count as "providing an estimate", and is "planning range" the right label?** This decides whether ST-1a's "we don't … give estimates" holds with the calculator live, and how ST-6 and ST-7 read. It also decides whether a range may appear at What we found, inside the request flow. *Default:* the label "Planning range, not a quote" (ST-7), with ST-6's proposed revision ("only a mover can price your move, in a written estimate"). *Blocks:* 8 pages (Moving costs, How movers charge, Calculator, Calculator method, Which companies, Movers vs brokers, How AMC makes money, Editorial policy).
3. **C3. Does "what a mover pays never changes its ratings or where it appears" hold, when only customers whose request a mover bought can review it?** Buying more requests brings more invitations, more reviews and earlier eligibility for rating-sort priority at `{{setting.rank_min_reviews}}`. Related: must a material-connection line sit beside each rating and the badge, and does "pays us" hold when a mover buys with promotional or goodwill credit? *Default:* the "never an input" variants in the ST-1 and ST-3 notes, and the "pays us" sentence in ST-5. *Blocks:* 7 pages (How AMC makes money, Review methodology, Editorial policy, Which companies, How we verify, Calculator and Moving costs through ST-1b and ST-1e).
4. **D1–D3 with O25. Can any cost range publish at launch?** No rate cards exist, the v1 method lives only in the drafts and build plan §7.3, and no one has defined the area the 5-mover threshold counts in, or the area behind each Moving costs row. Until these are settled, the Moving costs table, the calculator page and every range stay at the gate, and Moving costs likely misses Wave 1. *Default:* origin state as the area, the build plan §7.3 v1 method, and hold the pages until one area passes. *Blocks:* 4 pages (Moving costs, Calculator, Calculator method, How movers charge).
5. **O1. Who is the named author, the different reviewer and, on cost pages, the data owner, and what are the organization's legal name, address and contact addresses?** README §8 item 3 blocks every page until they are named (brief §11 Q1, Q9; implications §11 Q17). *Default:* none. *Blocks:* all ten.
