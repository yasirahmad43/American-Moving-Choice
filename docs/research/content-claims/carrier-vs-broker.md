# Carrier vs broker: regulatory claims check

**Prepared:** 2026-10-28. **Status:** research note for page writers, the owner and counsel. It is not legal advice and nothing in it is approved copy. **Pages that rely on it:** `/movers-vs-brokers/` and `/how-we-verify-movers/`.

## Header

**Purpose.** Check the regulatory claims (B1 to B19) that the two trust pages need about motor carriers, household goods (HHG) brokers, carrier agents, FMCSA records and broker duties, so the pages describe the rules accurately and link the right primary source. The note describes what the rules say. It draws no conclusions about American Moving Choice (AMC); those are questions under "Items for counsel".

**Method.**
- Started from `docs/research/compliance-and-platform-facts.md` ("compliance facts"), reused its facts and cited it by section. Searched again only for exact citations and wording.
- WebSearch, standard mode first and extended mode for thin items (B12, B16, B19, the 2022 survey rule), limited where possible to uscode.house.gov, ecfr.gov, govinfo.gov, federalregister.gov, fmcsa.dot.gov, safer.fmcsa.dot.gov, li-public.fmcsa.dot.gov, leg.state.fl.us, flsenate.gov and fdacs.gov. law.cornell.edu is treated as a mirror of official text. Trade press was searched only to look for news of an NPRM (B16).
- **Access date for every source: 2026-10-28.**
- Status key: **verified-primary** (primary text read), **verified-excerpt** (search excerpt of the primary page), **secondary-only**, **unverified**, **contradicted** (the correct statement follows).
- Excerpts are 25 words or fewer and copied from search excerpts. Where an excerpt joins two passages, "…" marks the gap.

Per the method, no other fetch was tried. So no row is verified-primary, and any page copy built on a row carries `[Verify: …]` until someone opens the cited page (content README §3 and §6). To fetch primary pages in a later session, the environment's network settings need `www.ecfr.gov` (and `federalregister.gov`, `fmcsa.dot.gov`, `uscode.house.gov`, `leg.state.fl.us`) under Allowed domains.

**What changes in compliance facts.**
- **B7:** the 50-mile survey radius in compliance facts §2 is out of date. A 2022 final rule removed it and allows surveys by video.
- **B11:** HHG *contract* carriers file liability insurance only; the cargo filing applies to HHG *common* carriers.
- **B14:** "NOT AUTHORIZED" is an operating-authority status, not a USDOT status. FMCSA has suspended "INACTIVE" for missed biennial updates due on or after 2026-06-01.
- **B15:** the USDOT suffixes exist (C, H, B and others) but show only for companies registering for the first time in Motus.
- **B17:** Florida's current definition of "moving broker" differs from the 2017 statute text compliance facts cites.
- **B19:** the quote and the citation (88 FR 39368–39373) check out against search excerpts.
