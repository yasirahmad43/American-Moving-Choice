### Operator and role (legal pages vs marketing)
- Operator named in both legal pages: "Marketing Monkeys Ltd." Privacy policy last updated Feb 6, 2026; terms August 2026. The privacy policy has GDPR-style sections ("Data Controller", "Legal Bases for Processing", "International Transfers").
- Site copy: "My Good Movers is an independent advertising and comparison platform based in the United States." · "My Good Movers is a comparison platform and does not provide moving services di[rectly]".
- Privacy policy: "My Good Movers is an independent online marketplace and lead-generation platform only." · "We do not provide, perform, arrange, broker, supervise, guarantee, or participate in moving…" · "We are not an agent, broker, carrier, employer, partner, or verifier for any moving company." · "We do not independently verify pricing, availability, service quality, licensing, insurance coverage, or performance."
- Terms: "Where the Platform displays a status such as "FMCSA registration confirmed" or a similar verification indicator next to a moving company's listing, that status reflects only the s[tatus]…"
