## Key observations (short quotes)

### Business model and disclosure
- `/disclosure/`: "To keep our site free for you, we provide phone numbers and links to sign up for some of the moving services featured in our reviews." · "When you click on these links or make a phone call, we may receive compensation."
- Paid placement labels: "Some of the placements you see on Move.org — such as "Preferred Partner" and "Featured Partner" — are sponsored. This means companies may pay to appear in a specific location on a page."
- "While our affiliate relationships may influence the moving services we review and write about and where they're placed on our site, it does not affect the analysis and opinions…"
- "In late 2024, Move.org adopted an updated, more rigorous editorial strategy." The page has an "AI statement" section.
- `/partnership/`: "our mission is to deliver qualified, ready-to-book leads that help our partners grow" · "Our rankings are assessed quarterly, and our annual Best Movers Awards recognize the best in the industry."
- Naming note for AMC: Move.org uses "Preferred Partner" for paid placements. Together with "Preferred Movers Network Inc." (owner of ConsumerMovingReviews), "preferred" already carries paid or affiliated meanings in this market.

### Best-of lists
- `/best-interstate-moving-companies/`: ranked list (1. Allied, 2. Colonial, 3. American Van Lines, 4. Mayzlin, 5. United) plus "BLVD Moving: Featured partner".
- Carrier nuance: "Every company below holds its own FMCSA carrier authority — none are brokers reselling your job…", followed by a caveat that some of them "handle your move…" with partner crews ("'Carrier' doesn't always mean one crew start to finish").
- Offers and phone numbers inside rankings: "🔥 Get up to $500 off*" · "Find-a-Mover tool Get matched with your best-fit mover Compare prices and availability Find My Match".
- Trust block: "movers helped every month 20k · moving grants awarded 24 · moving companies evaluated 156 · years of expert experience 80+".
- Bylines: "By Corey Whelan Sep 17, 2026 16 min read · Data Analysis By Daniel Delgado"; author bios ("At last count, Corey has moved 14 times").
