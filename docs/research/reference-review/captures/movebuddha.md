- "Each year, 400,000+ people trust our moving recommendations." · aggregates "over 1 million customer reviews from 25+ sources, including the Better Business Bureau."

- Lists "Industry Affiliations" (moving associations) and "Professional Memberships".

### Money and disclosure
- `/advertising-disclosure/`: "When you click on these links, submit your information via our webform, or make a phone call, we may receive compensation." · "While our affiliate relationships may influence moving services we review and write about and why they're placed on our site, it does not affect the analysis and opinions…"

- The same national partner cards appear on every city, state and route page captured: Safeway Moving ("best overall value", 4.9/5), American Van Lines ("most experienced mover", 4.9/5), North American Van Lines ("best customized plans"), each with a phone number and "get a quote".
