# MyGoodMovers: reference review

A moving comparison and lead-generation site whose long-distance quote page the owner listed as a reference [capture: /privacy-policy, /long-distance-moving/].

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-27. **Evidence:** 14 of 14 pages captured (main site plus quote landing host); guided walk of the /moving-cost-calculator form to the contact step; automated walk of /long-distance-moving/; Semrush; search. `/long-distance-moving/` and "quote-host root" are on quote.mygoodmovers.com, other paths on mygoodmovers.com. **Confidence:** medium, because the owner's named page had no working form and nothing after contact or on the mover side was seen.

## At a glance
- Quote pages state the role above the fold, but among captured pages only the legal pages say movers buy plans that can change listing order, with no sponsored label seen [capture: /long-distance-moving/, /terms-and-conditions, /movers/al].
- A four-step calculator gives no personal value before contact except the route distance [walk: /moving-cost-calculator].
- One unticked box bundles Terms acceptance with contact by unnamed "moving companies" [walk: /moving-cost-calculator].
- Vetting claims are disclaimed in the privacy policy [capture: /best-movers, /privacy-policy].
- One company shows different ratings and prices across pages [capture: quote-host root, /long-distance-moving/, /movers/al].
- About 25,778 estimated organic visits a month, 37% on one guide [semrush: domain_rank, top_pages_more].
- The owner's named page showed raw form code, not a form [walk: /long-distance-moving/].

- The FAQ says "Up to three moving companies may also contact you"; the consumer cannot choose the number, and the consent step omits it [capture, walk: /moving-cost-calculator].
- On /long-distance-moving/ and the quote-host root, "GET MY FREE QUOTE [mygood_movers_form]" shows as text and that button did nothing; six "GET QUOTE" buttons went untested [capture; walk].

## Mover side
The terms say movers "may purchase one of several paid subscription plans" that "can affect the order or prominence" of listings [capture: /terms-and-conditions]. The FAQ's limit of three implies shared leads [capture: /moving-cost-calculator]. Plan prices, lead caps, exclusivity and the "For Movers" page were not seen; search found nothing [capture; search]. AMC's lead preview with buyer count (lead-lifecycle §5) already discloses more than MGM's captured pages [inference].

## Trust, verification and reviews
- **Claims vs. fine print.** "1200+ FMCSA Verified Movers" (/) and "hand-vetted" (/best-movers) clash with "We do not independently verify … licensing, insurance coverage, or performance" (/privacy-policy) [capture]. The terms say a listing is no "endorsement, recommendation, or approval", yet /movers/al shows "Our Recommended Movers" [capture: /terms-and-conditions, /movers/al].
- **Cards.** Cards show the USDOT number, "FMCSA Active" and "Insurance Full Coverage", but no carrier or broker type and no check date [capture: /movers/al]. /movers calls the USDOT match a "License lookup" [capture], though registration is not operating authority (compliance facts §1).
- **Ranking.** /movers/al promises "Fair and unbiased mover rankings", yet gives "BEST LOCAL MOVER" and first rank to a mover with two reviews, above one with ten [capture]. International Van Lines leads three pages; whether that is paid is unknown [capture: quote-host root, /long-distance-moving/, /movers/al].
- **Consistency.** International Van Lines (USDOT 2293832 in FMCSA's record [search: ai.fmcsa.dot.gov, official, via excerpt]) shows "(4.9 MGM RATING)" on the quote-host root, "4.8/5" and "$7149.56" on /long-distance-moving/, and "$7,182" on /movers/al; no captured page defines the MGM rating or either price's scope [capture]. Every captured quote-host review card shows 4.4, even for a mover rated 4.8 on /movers [capture; inference: possibly a placeholder or per-review score]. Home and /movers agree for the four movers on both [capture].
- **Reviews.** /long-distance-moving/ credits "third-party sources", while the main site invites reviews and screens them "by hand and by algorithm" (/movers) [capture]. No captured page says what proof a reviewer gives [capture]. Home shows negative reviews with move cost and the mover's base state [capture: /].
- **Numbers and bylines.** "15,400 Verified Customer Reviews" (/) and "Reviews 10,000+" (/about-us) have no source or date [capture]. "Fact-Checked" stamps name no person [capture: /movers/al, /editorial-team].
- **Public record.** Searches found a Trustpilot score of about 4 from 5 reviews but no BBB profile, lawsuit or regulator action; that means unknown, not good, standing [search: br.trustpilot.com, third party; inference].

## Disclosure and consent
- **Role.** "My Good Movers is a comparison platform and does not provide moving services" sits above the fold [capture: /long-distance-moving/]. v1 (search-only) did not find it; the capture shows it now [v1; capture].
- **Mixed signals.** "Plan It, Compare It, and Book It" (/) sits beside "We do not … arrange, broker … or participate in moving" (/privacy-policy) [capture], a mixed impression [inference]. "Strong editorial independence" is claimed (/editorial-team), yet no captured ranking page says movers pay [capture: /movers, /movers/al].
- **Operator.** Both legal pages name "Marketing Monkeys Ltd.", the privacy policy has GDPR-style headings, and the captured footer says "based in the United States" with no postal address [capture: /privacy-policy, /long-distance-moving/]. A third-party profile lists a Florida address [search: jp.trustpilot.com, third party], and no source linked operator and brand [search].
- **Consent text.** "By submitting, you agree to our Terms & Privacy Policy and consent to be contacted by moving companies via call, text, or email." [walk: /moving-cost-calculator]. It names no mover and omits automated-technology, not-a-condition-of-purchase, message-rate and opt-out wording; counsel must judge it (compliance facts §3) [walk].
- **Data and tracking.** The privacy policy lists fields sent to movers, open-ended ("together with any additio…"), and the form shows no summary [capture: /privacy-policy; walk]. No consent-certificate script was detected, while ad pixels, Hotjar and Clarity load site-wide [capture].

## SEO and content
- **Traffic.** About 25,778 estimated visits a month come from 23,986 keywords and no paid ones [semrush: domain_rank]. The top two pages take 54.4%: /city-guide/where-should-i-move-in-the-usa (9,524) and /movers (4,496 from 1,483 keywords) [semrush: top_pages_more].
- **Families.** Informational guides hold about 53.7% and eight state pages 8.8% [semrush: top_pages_more]. No calculator, route, city, best-of or glossary URL makes the top 25, so each draws about 115 visits or fewer [semrush]. That refines the discovery brief's claim that competitors barely serve state hubs [inference].
- **Templates.** /movers/al runs 2,905 words, but its headings repeat a {State} pattern and its blurbs restate data fields [capture; inference: likely templated, but only one state page was captured]. /best-movers is 272 words, with title, H1 and H2 aimed at different intents [capture].
- **Other.** No standalone route page was observed [capture; semrush; v1]. FAQPage remains on /movers/al and /packing-calculator, and the two near-duplicate quote-host pages have no JSON-LD [capture].

## Design and UX
- The mobile home screen fits the H1, a two-field route starter and a reassurance line [capture: /].
- Quote landing pages drop site navigation and lead with quote buttons, plus a phone number on desktop [capture: /long-distance-moving/].
- Two-field starters sit on /movers/al, /popular-routes, /best-movers and the glossary, but the top traffic page was not captured [capture; semrush].

## What AMC should adopt or adapt
- **Role line in every quote hero** [capture: /long-distance-moving/]. Better: reuse one statement verbatim and add, there and in the survey, that movers pay AMC without affecting verification, ranking or ratings. Serves copy draft §1, §7 and site-architecture §7.1.
- **Short move-first steps** [walk: /moving-cost-calculator]. Better: show the labeled range and the coverage check before contact. Serves lead-lifecycle §2; an earlier mover count needs a visual-direction §13.7 change.
- **Channel-preference step** [walk: /moving-cost-calculator]. Better: carry it into consent scope, the lead record and the mover preview, so email-only means no calls. Serves compliance facts §3 and needs a lead-lifecycle §6 field.
- **Range label and method link at the tool** [capture: /moving-cost-calculator]. Better: add assumptions, sample size, an as-of date and an insufficient-data state, with no instant-price promise. Serves site-architecture §6.3 and compliance facts §2.
- **USDOT on every card** [capture: /movers]. Better: add MC, carrier authority, no broker authority, insurance filings and a dated SAFER link, naming each check accurately. Serves visual-direction §9.4 and mover-verification §3–§5.
- **Mixed-sentiment reviews with move facts** [capture: /]. Better: add evidence labels, dates, company responses and only move facts shared with consent. Serves mover-verification §6; using reviewer prices as cost data needs a site-architecture §6.3 change.
- **Answer-first state page with a saved shortlist** [capture: /movers/al]. Better: fill it with sourced state facts, and let a shortlist reorder, not choose, the movers named at consent. Serves site-architecture §4.1, §7.4 and mover-verification §5.
- **Directory hub and relocation demand** [semrush: top_pages_more; capture: /movers]. Better: give /movers/ dated verified counts and a method summary, and meet "where to move" demand with migration data, not place rankings. Serves site-architecture §1.2 and §7.5.

## What AMC should avoid
- Marketing claims the legal pages withdraw (mover-verification §4; site-architecture §7.2) [capture].
- Consent that bundles Terms, names no mover and leaves the company limit to an FAQ [walk]; AMC's draft has the same Terms bundle, so split it (copy draft §5 vs. compliance facts §3, lead-lifecycle §6).
- Terms allowing paid order with no visible label, beside fairness claims (compliance facts §4) [capture].
- A "best" label for a mover with two reviews (site-architecture §4.5–§4.6) [capture].
- Two-letter state slugs sharing a folder with company pages (site-architecture §0) [semrush: top_pages_more].
- Conflicting ratings or cent-level prices (site-architecture §6.3, §8.5) [capture].
- Undated counters and anonymous "Fact-Checked" stamps (site-architecture §7.1–§7.3) [capture].
- Booking language (compliance facts §2) [capture].
- Broken entry pages; check releases for raw template tokens and a working first step (site-architecture §3.3, G10) [walk].
- Thin mixed-intent "best" hubs, near-duplicate landing pages, and replay or pixels on form pages without a tag policy (site-architecture §3.3, §4.6, §8.6) [capture; inference].
