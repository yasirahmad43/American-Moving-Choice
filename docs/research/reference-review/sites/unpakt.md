# Unpakt: reference review

A moving marketplace where consumers compare "guaranteed" prices, book a mover and pay Unpakt, which pays the mover after the move [capture /, /how-it-works/]. It is on the list as the clearest case of the book-and-pay model AMC has ruled out [inference].

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-27. **Evidence:** 9 of 9 pages captured (www, and blog.unpakt.com marked "blog"); automated and guided walks of the home form, both stopped at the first screen; Semrush US, October 2026; earlier-stage WebSearch, not re-verified, so search-only claims are marked or left out. **Confidence:** medium, because nothing past the first form screen, and no profile or city page, was observed.

## At a glance
- Movers set a "cost per unit of stuff", a formula sets a "Guaranteed price", and the consumer pays Unpakt, which pays the mover [capture /how-it-works/, /].
- No captured page says how Unpakt is paid or how it checks movers, and none shows a USDOT or MC number [capture].
- All six captured www pages mark Unpakt up as a MovingCompany rated 4.2 from 36 ratings; no visible rating was recorded [capture].
- The first screen presets size and date; the guided walk then failed twice and landed on /sign-in/ [walk /].
- About 5,976 estimated organic visits a month, 85% on two URLs, and no top-10 rank in the 14 SERPs pulled for AMC terms [semrush domain_rank, top_pages_more, phrase_organic_top10].
- The model mirrors the "Booking deposits and payments" add-on still listed in AMC's mvp-scope §5 [capture /; inference].

## Mover side
Movers set their own unit cost [capture /how-it-works/]. No captured page covers mover fees, joining rules or screening, and the For Service Providers, Agreement and Terms pages were not captured [capture]. A 13% fee in 2012 press and help-center commission tiers were not re-verified [search: betakit.com, third party; unpakt-mover-faq.groovehq.com, site's own]. Referral partners, including real estate agents, earn a fee per booked move, "paid monthly", and may offer clients "an exclusive discount for any moving company th…" (cut off) [capture /partnership/, footer]. ## Trust, verification and reviews
- **Vetting.** "Pre screened" (/partnership/) and "licensed" (/faq/) come with no method [capture]. v1's license, insurance, registration and BBB checks are unconfirmed [v1 §2.7].
- **Guarantee.** The home page offers a "100% price and service guarantee", says quotes are "not based on estimates" and promises "no hidden fees" [capture /]. The price comes instantly from the online inventory, before anyone sees the goods; later surveys were not observed [capture /how-it-works/].
- **Claims.** "800,000+ moves booked", "save 45%", "the only place" and "the the biggest online resource" have no source or date [capture /, /partnership/, blog / meta]. v1's 2015 commissioned-study origin for the 45% is unconfirmed [v1 §2.7].
- **Ratings.** The 4.2 / 36 markup describes Unpakt itself, even on /sign-in/ [capture]; 36 ratings beside 800,000+ bookings looks hard-coded [inference]. Off-site scores were not re-verified, so none is repeated [search, third party].
- **Reviews.** "Real customer reviews" are promised, but no captured page explains collection or checks [capture / meta]. v1 said some profile reviews come from other portals [v1 §2.7].

## Disclosure and consent
- **Role.** The copy says "our movers" yet presents a booking platform, and no captured page says whether Unpakt is a mover, broker or neither [capture /]. Holding payments sits uneasily with v1's report that the Agreement disclaims "involvement or control over the actual transaction" [inference; v1 §2.7].
- **Payment.** "You don't pay the movers directly" [capture /]. FMCSA's 2023 guidance weighs who handles money between shipper and carrier (compliance facts §2).
- **Consent.** No consent wording or consent-certificate script was seen, but the contact step was never reached [walk; capture]. An "I want to connect utilities" option sits by the main button, its type and default unrecorded [capture /].
- **Tags.** Google Ads, Tag Manager, Facebook, Zoho PageSense and Cloudflare load; no cookie-consent host appears, though one could be self-hosted [capture].
- **Privacy.** The "Unpakt, LLC" policy counts "its affiliates" as "we", calls "item or shipment values" non-personal and may obtain data from "commercially available sources" and ad networks; the excerpts do not say what movers receive [capture /privacy/].

## SEO and content
- **Footprint.** The home page takes 65.72% of estimated visits and /moving-companies/NY/new-york/ 19.29% [semrush top_pages_more]. With no top-10 rank for any AMC term pulled, it is no Wave 1 rival [semrush phrase_organic_top10].
- **Value.** Traffic cost per visit, about $22.94, is the highest in the reference set, twice hireahelper.com's $10.45 [semrush domain_rank]; commercial landing pages may explain it [inference].
- **City pages.** NYC earns 1,153 visits; five others earn 9 to 48, even at KD 37 (Miami) and KD 30 (Columbus) [semrush top_pages_more, phrase_these]. NYC has 4 times Miami's demand but 24 times its visits, for reasons the data does not show [semrush; site-architecture §5.2]. Profiles and service pages earn 9 to 73 [semrush].
- **URLs.** Uppercase states mix with lowercase cities (/moving-companies/FL/miami/), and profiles sit at /movers/{company}-{city}-{st}/ [semrush]. This corrects v1, which saw no geo URLs [v1 §2.7].
- **Two hosts.** www (MovingCompany markup) and a WordPress blog (Organization markup) have separate navs [capture]. NYC intent overlaps: the www city page and a blog "Best Moving Companies in New York City" post [capture blog /; semrush].
- **Blog.** Seven blog URLs earn 473 visits, about 330 on population, museum, restaurant and home-type lists [semrush]. Dates are relative ("2 years ago" to "4 years ago"), a title says "in 2023", and cost posts show no method or source [capture blog].
- **Markup.** /faq/ is 212 words of "READ MORE" links; no captured page has FAQPage, BreadcrumbList, Article or Person markup, or names an author or methodology page [capture]. The clearest explainer is the one-line price formula [capture /how-it-works/].
- **Hygiene.** /sign-in/ copies the home title and meta, the blog H1 is an image, and four typos appear [capture]. Robots directives were not captured [capture].
- **Local facts.** The blog home states a checkable NYC rule on certificates of insurance for co-ops and condos [capture blog /].

## Design and UX
- On mobile, "The Moving App" link precedes the H1 on all four www pages with a recorded order; the home "Moving From" field and a support link sit above the fold [capture /, /partnership/, /how-it-works/, /sign-in/].
- On desktop, promo, provider and login links precede the H1; the form's position was not recorded [capture /].
- Explainers carry a "Start!" form, and both home address inputs share one element id [capture /how-it-works/, /faq/; walk /].

## What AMC should adopt or adapt
- **Move details first, from any page** [walk /; capture /how-it-works/]. Better: route fields after the H1 and lede on mobile and every explainer; required size choices pairing bedrooms with square feet, never preset; a flexible-date option; stops and storage early; unique field ids and labels. Serves: quote survey (lead-lifecycle §2 steps 1-5; shared-pricing §6; visual-direction §12).
- **Price explainer** [capture /how-it-works/]. Better: a plain formula of the estimator's inputs on a versioned method page, shown as a range, with steps stating the exclusive and shared rule; an optional inventory step can sharpen the lead spec. Serves: value before contact (site-architecture §6.3 rule 5; reference-matrix §3).
- **Control, not a no-calls promise** [capture /]. Better: the consumer picks how many movers and sees who they are, with verification dates, before agreeing. Serves: quote survey (lead-lifecycle §2 steps 7-8).
- **Payment reassurance, turned around** [capture /]. Better: "How AMC makes money" and "Movers vs Brokers" pages saying movers pay for requests, consumers pay nothing and AMC never prices, books or takes payment, repeated by quote buttons and at confirmation; plus education on estimate types, the 110% rule and Protect Your Move's deposit red flags. Serves: trust pages (consent draft §1, §7; compliance facts §1 implication 7, §2).
- **Benefit block** [walk /]. Better: provable claims only (movers-only, you choose how many, names before consent). Serves: trust (visual-direction §12).
- **Real reviews** [capture / meta]. Better: evidence labels, separate quote and move ratings, a public methodology, and negative reviews with replies. Serves: reviews (mover-verification §6; compliance facts §4).
- **City data** [capture blog /category/moving-cost/, blog /]. Better: cost ranges only from mover rate cards (5+ movers) or cited studies, dated, on the page they describe, plus sourced building-access facts. Serves: real-data rule (site-architecture §4.1 G3-G5, §6.3 rule 2).
- **Joining-rule options** [inference]. Own trucks, which screens out brokers, and a minimum operating history, which screens out new or reincarnated carriers. Serves: movers only (mover-verification §1, §3).
- **Partner channel, later** [capture /partnership/]. Better: the same survey, named-mover consent and sharing limit; partner disclosure is a counsel question; reward email forms stay apart from quote consent; widget links are plain and marked sponsored [verify]. Serves: lead-lifecycle §2; compliance facts §3-§4; mvp-scope §5.

## What AMC should avoid
- Pricing, booking, taking payment, or guaranteeing or discounting movers' prices (discovery brief §1; compliance facts §2) [capture /, /partnership/]. Flag the mvp-scope §5 payments add-on for removal or counsel sign-off (compliance facts §7 implication 4; discovery brief §4).
- Copy that sets movers' prices apart from estimates or promises "no hidden fees" (consent draft §6) [capture /].
- Mover-type markup, "our movers" and self-ratings; use one Organization @id, mover types only for movers, aggregateRating only on profiles with on-page reviews, and a schema lint (site-architecture §7.1, §8.5, §9; compliance facts §5) [capture].
- Silence on who pays; publish mover terms at /for-movers/, and never call reviews "independent" without saying movers pay (compliance facts §4, §465.6 risk) [capture].
- Unsourced counts and savings, and "best", "only", "biggest" or "guaranteed" claims; define and date counts, and ask counsel about substantiation (reference-matrix §4 item 7; site-architecture §4.6) [capture /].
- Unexplained vetting; show USDOT and MC with FMCSA links, and link badges to a dated method page (compliance facts §1 implication 6; mover-verification §4, §7) [capture].
- Calling move details non-personal or sharing with unnamed "affiliates"; name the buying movers and what they get (lead-lifecycle §5), and have counsel's privacy review cover them (backlog line 79) [capture /privacy/].
- Cross-sells without owner and counsel sign-off (consent draft §3; lead-lifecycle §1), tags that read form fields (site-architecture §8.6) and leads enriched from commercial sources [capture].
- Errors that drop answers or force a login; requesting quotes needs no account (lead-lifecycle §1-2) [walk].
- City pages without coverage and data (gates G1, G4, G5); enter New York through routes and long distance, not "movers nyc" (KD 72) (site-architecture §4.1, §5.3) [semrush].
- A second host, uppercase states, lifestyle posts, link-only FAQs, relative dates and years in titles; use absolute "Last updated" dates (site-architecture §2.1-§2.2, §7.3-§7.4, §8.2) [capture; semrush].
- Duplicate titles, image H1s and app links on the mobile first screen (site-architecture §9; visual-direction §12) [capture].

## Gaps and open questions
- Everything after the first screen: contact step, account rules, how many movers are priced, consent and named sellers [walk].
- Agreement, Terms and For Service Providers pages: arbitration, refunds, card timing, commission disclosure [capture].
- How Unpakt is paid; FMCSA or state registration (one search found none, which proves nothing); BBB and complaint records [search].
- Profile and city templates; sources of the rating, scale and 45% claims; the utilities partner; the full privacy policy; whether tags receive form values [capture].
- Search summaries, not verified, link the founder to FlatRate Moving; whether related movers appear in comparisons is unknown [search].
- Robots, sitemaps, keyword lists, backlinks and AI answers [capture; semrush].
- Owner questions: merge "best movers in {city}" into city pages, against site-architecture §2.4 rule 6? A guide review interval tied to "Last reviewed" (§4.4, §7.3)? [inference]
