# Consumer Moving Reviews: reference review

An editorial site that ranks long-distance movers, publishes route guides and runs a quote-matching form [capture /, /request-a-quote/]. It is on the list as the closest model for a published ranking method and badge explainers, and as a warning about ownership conflicts [inference].

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-27. **Evidence:** 10 of 10 pages captured; guided walk of `/request-a-quote/` to the contact step (step 2 of 2), nothing submitted; one automated estimator walk; Semrush US (domain summary, top 15 pages); six WebSearch runs, with unconfirmed results marked or left out. **Confidence:** medium, because nothing after submit was seen, and no profile, review, legal or mover page was captured.

## At a glance
- CMR says it is "an editorial review site, not a mover", yet collects contact details for "a vetted moving specialist" [capture, walk /request-a-quote/].
- No captured text says how it earns money; only a meta description mentions "advertiser compensation", which the page body contradicts [capture /advertiser-disclosure/].
- It names its owner, Preferred Movers Network Inc., and two "related companies" that lead both captured best-of quick answers [capture /best-interstate-moving-companies/, /best-movers-for-small-long-distance-moves/].
- Contact comes on screen 2, before any price, mover name or company count; consent is one "By submitting" line [walk, capture /request-a-quote/].
- Worth adapting: dated FMCSA checks, USDOT and carrier labels in list rows, explained badges, a published method [capture /best-interstate-moving-companies/, /how-we-rank-movers/].
- About 191 estimated organic visits a month, about 85% of them to one company profile [semrush domain_rank, top_pages_more].

## Trust, verification and reviews
- **Conflict.** "CMR is owned by Preferred Movers Network Inc."; A Plus Moving and Storage and Jacob and Sons are "related companies" (the capture cuts the sentence off) [capture /best-interstate-moving-companies/]. It adds "Each holds its own USDOT number and is scored on its own record." [capture]. The quick answer just below puts A Plus first (10.0, the only 10.0 seen) and Jacob and Sons (9.4) above MC Movers (9.6) [capture]. The small-moves page leads with the same two, gives Jacob and Sons a single "quotes from $1,799.99" price and shows no captured disclosure [capture /best-movers-for-small-long-distance-moves/].
- **Badges and licensing.** A gold "Certified Approved seal" and a teal "Verified mark" each get a one-line meaning, and CMR says neither is paid [capture /how-we-rank-movers/, /advertiser-disclosure/]. Lists cite an FMCSA check "on Oct 23, 2026", show rows like "Carrier · USDOT 3006268", label brokers and keep lower scorers for lookup [capture /best-interstate-moving-companies/, /best-movers-for-small-long-distance-moves/]. CMR says featured movers show a BBB grade; no MC numbers appeared [capture /how-we-rank-movers/].
- **Method.** Five pillars, 3-to-6-month re-checks and "No invented data" are published; bylines name only teams, and /about/ words the criteria differently [capture /editorial-methodology/, /, /about/, /best-interstate-moving-companies/].
- **Numbers.** Home counters (8,200+ moves, 900+ profiles) show no captured source or date; the 29 verified interstate movers are about 3% of the profiles [capture /, /best-interstate-moving-companies/; inference: arithmetic]. The quote form takes only moves "OVER 250 MILES", while the interstate page cites a mover (E.E. Ward) for "about 400 miles" and recommends only scores of 7.0 or higher [capture]. The small-moves page, where no threshold was captured, names Bekins (6.9) "Best guaranteed delivery date" [capture].
- **Reviews.** A sitewide "Write a Review" link exists, but the form was not captured; third-party feedback feeds an editorial 1-to-10 score, and no Review or AggregateRating type was among top-level markup types [capture /how-we-rank-movers/, /editorial-methodology/].

## Disclosure and consent
- **Role and money.** The role line sits below the form [capture /request-a-quote/]. /advertiser-disclosure/ says "There are no paid placements on CMR." and makes referral fees hypothetical, where v1 had "may earn" [capture; v1 §2.1].
- **Consent.** "By submitting, you agree to be contacted by a vetted moving specialist. We never sell your info." sits under the step-1 button; step 2 showed no checkbox and no call or text wording, and no arbitration wording appeared [capture, walk /request-a-quote/]. It lacks the written-consent elements in compliance facts §3, which may or may not apply to the unobserved calls [inference].
- **Sharing.** "Never sell" and "Private by default" stand without a captured privacy policy, so recipients are unknown [capture /request-a-quote/, /advertiser-disclosure/].
- **Broker-like wording.** The "accurate quote" line, moves "booked through the CMR network", "2,046 BOOKED MOVES PRICED" and matching to "the top-rated carriers" describe pricing, booking and judgment [walk; capture /best-movers-for-small-long-distance-moves/, /, /request-a-quote/]. No USDOT or MC number for CMR appeared [capture]. Compliance facts §2 treats these as broker risks; this is a pattern, not a finding that CMR is a broker.
- **Tracking.** Tag Manager, Clarity session replay, Bing, Facebook, Google Ads and a bzrcdn.openai.com script load; no consent-certificate script was detected [capture].

## SEO and content
- **Traffic.** An estimated 191 visits a month from 879 keywords, worth $187, with no paid keywords: a tenth of Budget Van Lines' 1,934 visits despite more keywords [semrush domain_rank; keyword-research §1]. CMR is no SEO model, and AMC should expect near-zero launch traffic [inference].
- **Profiles.** /companies/jason-jasons-relocations/ brings 162 visits (84.81%) from 2 keywords; profiles take 177 of 191 [semrush top_pages_more]. The unpulled keywords are most likely the company's name [inference].
- **Routes.** Four ZIP-to-ZIP lanes earn 1 to 3 visits each [semrush top_pages_more]. /routes/state/ca/ lists title-cased anchors ("Fresno Ca 93722 To Inman Sc 29349") under generic H2s, while /routes/ promises data "for that exact move"; no lane page was captured [capture].
- **Best-of pages.** At least 10 overlapping lists (Interstate, Long Distance, Nationwide, Cross-Country…) fill the home menu, as v1 warned [capture /; v1 §2.1]. The captured two run 9,708 and 12,344 words and miss the top 15, which reaches 0-visit rows [capture; semrush].
- **Shapes and markup.** The interstate page leads with a quick answer and adds a jump index and a plain definition; its disclosure is a separate block a quote can drop [capture /best-interstate-moving-companies/; inference]. FAQPage stays on 4 of 10 pages, and Article marks up hubs [capture].
- **Other.** An "of the month" label clashes with 3-to-6-month re-checks, and car-shipping pages and the promoted "2026 Moving Cost Report" earn almost nothing [capture /; semrush].

## Design and UX
On mobile, /request-a-quote/ opens on the form with no H1 above the fold [capture]. On the four desktop pages recorded, "(888) 695-0663" precedes "Get a Quote"; best-of pages pair "Get free quotes" with a call line [capture /, /best-interstate-moving-companies/]. Best-of pages repeat inline ZIP forms, and one table says "Scroll sideways on a phone." [capture /best-movers-for-small-long-distance-moves/].

## What AMC should adopt or adapt
- **Dated licensing rows.** Better: USDOT (and MC where held), an FMCSA link and verified-on date on every listing, rechecked weekly; exclude brokers and offer a lookup tool. Serves movers-only (mover-verification §3, §5).
- **Badge explainer.** Better: one "Preferred Mover — American Moving Choice" badge with a clipboard-check glyph and date, never a seal or "certified". Serves the badge (copy draft §8; visual-direction §3, §8).
- **Published method.** Better: one versioned method with identical criteria names, "data as of" dates, "insufficient data" labels, an FMCSA data-sources page and named authors. Serves trust pages (site-architecture §7.2–§7.3; reference-matrix §3).
- **Short first screen and honeypot.** Better: move details and the coverage pre-check before contact, with honest step copy. Serves the survey order (lead-lifecycle §2).
- **Value before contact.** Better: a labeled range and matched-mover preview inside the survey, pre-filled from the calculator. Serves that goal (site-architecture §6.3; compliance facts §2).
- **Inventory step.** Better: preparation for the mover's own survey, never a sight-unseen estimate. Serves lead quality (compliance facts §2).
- **Answer-first rankings.** Better: the material-connection line inside the ranking block, FMCSA-sourced definitions and stacked cards on phones. Serves SEO (site-architecture §4.6, §7.4; visual-direction §12).
- **Profiles as early traffic.** Better: prioritize the completeness gate; a profile-started request names that company first while the consumer picks the count. Serves profiles (mover-verification §5; copy draft §4) [inference].

## What AMC should avoid
- Ranking or featuring companies tied to AMC or its staff. Add a related-party check in Admin review and disclose ties on profiles; badge eligibility is an owner decision and the FTC question is for counsel (mover-verification §4; compliance facts §4).
- Role and money lines kept to trust pages, or "independent" claims. Show the role and money lines at survey step 1, consent and confirmation, from one text that also feeds meta tags (copy draft §1, §7).
- "By submitting" consent. Use the unticked named-mover box (automated calls and texts, not a condition, STOP, no arbitration), a stored consent record and masked replay (copy draft §5; compliance facts §3, §8).
- Absolute "never sell" promises; flag copy draft §3's no-sale sentence for counsel.
- Quote, booking or "right carrier" wording, staff sales calls and sales phone CTAs (compliance facts §2; lead-lifecycle §3).
- Hidden company counts and speed promises (lead-lifecycle §2; visual-direction §13.8).
- ZIP lane pages, parallel best-of lists and shipping verticals (site-architecture §0, §3.1, §4.1).
- Drifting numbers: keep one record per score, count and threshold for body, meta and JSON-LD; no unsourced counters, member "from" prices, fake freshness or FAQPage markup (site-architecture §6.3, §7.3, §8.5, §9).
- Open review buttons; invite only consumers whose request a mover bought (mover-verification §6).
- An unchecked "Preferred" name: CMR's owner and Move.org's paid "Preferred Partner" label [capture moveorg.md] blur it, so clear the trademark first (counsel).
