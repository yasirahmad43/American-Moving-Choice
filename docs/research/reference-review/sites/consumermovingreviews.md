# Consumer Moving Reviews: reference review

An editorial site that ranks long-distance movers, publishes route guides and runs a quote-matching form [capture /, /request-a-quote/]. It is on the list as the closest model for a published ranking method and badge explainers, and as a warning about ownership conflicts [inference].

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-27. **Evidence:** 10 of 10 pages captured; guided walk of `/request-a-quote/` to the contact step (step 2 of 2), nothing submitted; one automated estimator walk; Semrush US (domain summary, top 15 pages); six WebSearch runs, with unconfirmed results marked or left out. **Confidence:** medium, because nothing after submit, and no profile, review, legal or mover page, was seen.

## At a glance
- CMR says it is "an editorial review site, not a mover", yet collects contact details for "a vetted moving specialist" [capture, walk /request-a-quote/].
- No captured text says how it earns money. Only a meta description mentions "advertiser compensation", which the page body denies [capture /advertiser-disclosure/].
- It names its owner, Preferred Movers Network Inc., and two "related companies" that lead both captured best-of quick answers [capture /best-interstate-moving-companies/, /best-movers-for-small-long-distance-moves/].
- Contact comes on screen 2, before any price, mover name or company count, and consent is one "By submitting" line with no checkbox [walk, capture /request-a-quote/].
- Worth adapting: dated FMCSA checks, USDOT and carrier or broker labels, explained badges and a published method [capture /best-interstate-moving-companies/, /how-we-rank-movers/].
- About 191 estimated organic visits a month, 85% of them to one company profile [semrush domain_rank, top_pages_more].

## Trust, verification and reviews
- **Conflict.** "CMR is owned by Preferred Movers Network Inc."; A Plus Moving and Storage and Jacob and Sons are "related companies", in a sentence the capture cuts off [capture /best-interstate-moving-companies/]. It adds "Each holds its own USDOT number and is scored on its own record." [capture]. The quick answer just below ranks A Plus first at 10.0, the only 10.0 seen, and Jacob and Sons (9.4) ahead of MC Movers (9.6) [capture]. The small-moves page leads with the same two, shows Jacob and Sons "quotes from $1,799.99" and has no captured disclosure [capture /best-movers-for-small-long-distance-moves/].
- **Badges and licensing.** A gold "Certified Approved seal" and a teal "Verified mark" each get a one-line meaning, and CMR says neither is paid [capture /how-we-rank-movers/, /advertiser-disclosure/]. Lists cite an FMCSA authority check "on Oct 23, 2026", label rows "Carrier · USDOT 3006268" or broker, and keep lower scorers listed for lookup [capture /best-interstate-moving-companies/, /best-movers-for-small-long-distance-moves/]. CMR says featured movers show a BBB grade; no MC numbers appeared [capture /how-we-rank-movers/].
- **Method.** Five pillars, re-checks "every 3 to 6 months" and a "No invented data" rule are published, but bylines name teams, not people, and /about/ words the criteria differently [capture /editorial-methodology/, /, /about/, /best-interstate-moving-companies/].
- **Numbers.** Home counters (8,200+ moves tracked, 900+ profiles) show no captured source or date, and the 29 verified interstate movers are about 3% of the profiles [capture /, /best-interstate-moving-companies/; inference: arithmetic]. The quote form takes only moves "OVER 250 MILES", while the interstate page puts long distance at "about 400 miles", citing a mover (E.E. Ward) [capture]. That page recommends only scores of 7.0 or higher; the small-moves page states no threshold and names Bekins (6.9) "Best guaranteed delivery date" [capture]. v1's score and BBB-grade conflicts were not re-confirmed [v1 §2.1].
- **Reviews.** A sitewide "Write a Review" link exists, but the form was not captured. Third-party feedback feeds an editorial 1-to-10 score, and no Review or AggregateRating type was recorded [capture /how-we-rank-movers/, /editorial-methodology/].

## Disclosure and consent
- **Role and money.** The role line sits below the form, under the fourth H2 [capture /request-a-quote/]. /advertiser-disclosure/ says "There are no paid placements on CMR." and treats referral fees as hypothetical, where v1 had "may earn a referral fee" [capture; v1 §2.1].
- **Consent.** "By submitting, you agree to be contacted by a vetted moving specialist. We never sell your info." sits under the step-1 button [capture /request-a-quote/]. Step 2 showed no checkbox and no call or text wording, and no arbitration wording was seen [walk]. The line lacks the written-consent elements in compliance facts §3; whether they applied depends on calls that were not observed [inference].
- **Sharing.** "We never sell your info" and "Private by default" stand without a captured privacy policy, so recipients are unknown [capture /request-a-quote/, /advertiser-disclosure/].
- **Broker-like wording.** The "accurate quote" line, moves "booked through the CMR network" and "2,046 BOOKED MOVES PRICED" describe pricing and booking, and matching to "the top-rated carriers" is framed as CMR's judgment [walk; capture /best-movers-for-small-long-distance-moves/, /, /request-a-quote/]. No USDOT or MC number for CMR appeared [capture]. Compliance facts §2 treats these as broker risks; this is a pattern, not a finding that CMR is a broker.
- **Tracking.** Tag Manager, Clarity session replay, Bing, Facebook, Google Ads and a bzrcdn.openai.com script load; no consent-certificate script was detected [capture].

## SEO and content
- **Traffic.** CMR gets 191 visits a month from 879 keywords, worth $187, with no paid keywords: a tenth of Budget Van Lines' 1,934 visits, despite more keywords [semrush domain_rank; keyword-research §1].
- **Profiles.** /companies/jason-jasons-relocations/ brings 162 visits (84.81%) from 2 keywords, and profiles take 177 of 191 [semrush top_pages_more]. The keywords were not pulled; they are most likely the company's name [inference].
- **Routes.** Four ZIP-to-ZIP lanes earn 1 to 3 visits each [semrush top_pages_more]. /routes/state/ca/ lists title-cased anchors ("Fresno Ca 93722 To Inman Sc 29349") under generic H2s, while /routes/ promises data "for that exact move"; no lane page was captured [capture].
- **Best-of pages.** The home menu lists at least 10, including Interstate, Long Distance, Nationwide and Cross-Country, confirming v1's overlap concern [capture /; v1 §2.1]. The captured two run 9,708 and 12,344 words and miss the top 15, which reaches 0-visit rows [capture; semrush].
- **Shapes and markup.** The interstate page opens with a quick answer, jump index and plain definition, but its disclosure is a separate block a quote can drop [capture /best-interstate-moving-companies/; inference]. FAQPage stays on 4 of 10 pages, and Article marks up hubs [capture].
- **Other.** An "of the month" label clashes with 3-to-6-month re-checks [capture /]. URLs include a 2-letter state hub, a "-3" slug suffix and a HireAHelper profile [capture /routes/state/ca/; semrush]. Car-shipping URLs and the promoted "2026 Moving Cost Report" earn little or nothing measurable [semrush; capture /].
- **For AMC.** Treat CMR as a model for trust, not SEO, and start with low-difficulty families such as "usdot number lookup" (KD 18) and state routes (KD 10–25) [semrush keyword-research §3].

## Design and UX
On mobile, /request-a-quote/ opens on the form with no H1 above the fold [capture]. On the four desktop pages recorded, "(888) 695-0663" precedes "Get a Quote", and best-of pages pair "Get free quotes" with a call line [capture /, /best-interstate-moving-companies/]. Best-of pages repeat inline ZIP forms, and one table says "Scroll sideways on a phone." [capture /best-movers-for-small-long-distance-moves/].

## What AMC should adopt or adapt
- **Dated licensing rows.** Better: USDOT (and MC where held), an FMCSA link and verified-on date on every listing, rechecked weekly, with brokers excluded and a lookup tool for quoted movers. Serves movers-only (mover-verification §3, §5).
- **Badge explainer.** Better: one "Preferred Mover — American Moving Choice" badge with a clipboard-check glyph and date, never a seal or "certified". Serves the badge rules (copy draft §8; visual-direction §3, §8).
- **Published method.** Better: one versioned method page with identical criteria names everywhere, "data as of" dates, "insufficient data" labels, an FMCSA data-sources page and named authors. Serves trust pages (site-architecture §7.2–§7.3; reference-matrix §3).
- **Short first screen and honeypot.** Better: move details and the coverage pre-check before contact, with honest step copy. Serves the survey order (lead-lifecycle §2).
- **Value before contact.** Better: a labeled range and matched-mover preview inside the survey, pre-filled from the calculator. Serves the value-before-contact goal (site-architecture §6.3; compliance facts §2).
- **Inventory step.** Better: preparation for the mover's own survey, never a sight-unseen estimate. Serves lead quality (compliance facts §2).
- **Answer-first rankings.** Better: the material-connection line inside the ranking block, FMCSA-sourced definitions and stacked cards on phones. Serves SEO and AI visibility (site-architecture §4.6, §7.4; visual-direction §12).
- **Profiles as early traffic.** Better: prioritize the completeness gate; a profile-started request names that company first while the consumer picks the count. Serves profiles (mover-verification §5; copy draft §4) [inference].

## What AMC should avoid
- Ranking or featuring companies tied to AMC or its staff. Add a related-party check in Admin review and disclose ties on profiles; badge eligibility is an owner decision, and the FTC question is for counsel (mover-verification §4; compliance facts §4).
- Role and money lines kept to trust pages, or "independent" claims. Show both at survey step 1, consent and confirmation, from one text that also feeds meta descriptions (copy draft §1, §7).
- "By submitting" consent. Use the unticked named-mover box (automated calls and texts, not a condition, STOP, no arbitration), a stored consent record and masked replay (copy draft §5; compliance facts §3, §8).
- Absolute "never sell" promises. Flag copy draft §3's no-sale sentence for counsel.
- Quote, booking or "right carrier" wording, staff sales calls and sales phone CTAs (compliance facts §2; lead-lifecycle §3).
- Hidden company counts and speed promises (lead-lifecycle §2; visual-direction §13.8).
- ZIP lane pages, parallel best-of lists and shipping verticals (site-architecture §0, §3.1, §4.1).
- Drifting numbers. Keep one record per score, count and threshold for body, meta and JSON-LD; no unsourced counters, member "from" prices, fake freshness or FAQPage markup (site-architecture §6.3, §7.3, §8.5, §9).
- Open review buttons. Invite only consumers whose request a mover bought (mover-verification §6).
- An unchecked "Preferred" name. CMR's owner and Move.org's paid "Preferred Partner" label [capture moveorg.md] blur it, so clear the trademark first (site-architecture §1.2; counsel).
