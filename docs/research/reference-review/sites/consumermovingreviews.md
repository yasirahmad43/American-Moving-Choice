# Consumer Moving Reviews: reference review

An editorial site that ranks long-distance movers, publishes route guides and runs a quote-matching form [capture /, /request-a-quote/]. It is on the list as the closest reference for a published ranking method and badge explainers, and as a warning about ownership conflicts [inference].

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-27. **Evidence:** 10 of 10 pages captured; guided walk of `/request-a-quote/` to the contact step (step 2 of 2), with no contact details entered and nothing submitted; automated walk of the homepage estimator (one input); Semrush US, October 2026 (domain summary and top 15 pages); six single-run WebSearch queries, whose unconfirmed results are marked or left out. **Confidence:** medium, because nothing after "Get my matches" was seen, and no profile, review, legal or mover-facing page was captured.

## At a glance
- CMR calls itself "an editorial review site, not a mover", yet its quote form collects contact details for "a vetted moving specialist" [capture, walk /request-a-quote/].
- No captured text says how CMR earns money. Only the disclosure page's meta description mentions "advertiser compensation", which the page body denies [capture /advertiser-disclosure/].
- CMR names its owner as Preferred Movers Network Inc. Two movers it calls "related companies" lead the quick answer on both captured best-of pages, and one holds the only 10.0 seen [capture /best-interstate-moving-companies/, /best-movers-for-small-long-distance-moves/].
- Contact details come on screen 2, before any price, mover name or company count. Up to that point the consumer is never asked how many companies may call [walk /request-a-quote/].
- Consent is one "By submitting" line: no checkbox, no named seller, no automated-call, text or opt-out wording [capture, walk /request-a-quote/].
- Trust shapes worth adapting: a dated FMCSA check, USDOT and a carrier or broker label per row, two badges with one-line meanings, and a published five-pillar method [capture /best-interstate-moving-companies/, /how-we-rank-movers/, /editorial-methodology/].
- About 191 estimated organic visits a month, about 85% of them to one company profile [semrush domain_rank, top_pages_more].

## Mover side
The captured header and footer have no "for movers", "advertise" or "list your company" link, and no join path, lead prices or sharing rules appeared [capture, navigation]. The interstate page says "No mover pays to be listed or ranked." It discloses consulting work by "some people behind CMR", the owner and two related movers, but describes no paid relationship with movers [capture /best-interstate-moving-companies/]. Small-move prices partly come from moves "booked through the CMR network", and who booked them is unknown [capture /best-movers-for-small-long-distance-moves/]. AMC should do the opposite: a public "For moving companies" path, published pricing rules and factors, the sharing cap and credit policy (shared-pricing §3), and a conflicts register in the admin portal.

## Trust, verification and reviews
- **Conflict.** "CMR is owned by Preferred Movers Network Inc.", and A Plus Moving and Storage and Jacob and Sons are "related companies". The capture cuts that sentence off, so whom they are related to is not shown [capture /best-interstate-moving-companies/]. The next line reads "Each holds its own USDOT number and is scored on its own record." [capture /best-interstate-moving-companies/]. That block sits just above the quick answer, which names A Plus "Best overall" (10.0) and Jacob and Sons (9.4) ahead of MC Movers (9.6) [capture]. The small-moves page leads with the same two and shows Jacob and Sons "quotes from $1,799.99"; no related-company line was captured there [capture /best-movers-for-small-long-distance-moves/].
- **Badges.** The gold "CMR Certified Approved seal" means a mover "cleared our full review in all five areas"; the teal "CMR Verified mark" confirms "a real, licensed company" [capture /how-we-rank-movers/]. CMR says no company pays for either [capture /advertiser-disclosure/]. No guarantee or protection program appeared in the captured excerpts [capture].
- **Licensing.** Listed movers "held active interstate household goods authority on FMCSA when we checked on Oct 23, 2026", rows read like "Certified Approved · Carrier · USDOT 3006268", and brokers are labeled [capture /best-interstate-moving-companies/, /best-movers-for-small-long-distance-moves/]. Lower scorers stay listed "so you can look up any mover you were quoted" [capture /best-interstate-moving-companies/]. Captured rows show no MC numbers. CMR says every featured mover lists a BBB grade [capture /how-we-rank-movers/].
- **Method.** CMR publishes five pillars, a recency section, re-checks "every 3 to 6 months" and a "No invented data" rule [capture /editorial-methodology/, /how-we-rank-movers/, /, /about/]. Bylines name teams, not people, and /about/ lists different criteria from /editorial-methodology/ [capture].
- **Numbers.** Home counters (8,200+ moves tracked, 900+ profiles, 3,000+ pages) had no source or date captured beside them [capture /]. The interstate list covers 29 verified interstate movers, about 3% of the 900+ profiles; how many other movers are verified was not captured [capture /best-interstate-moving-companies/; inference: arithmetic]. The quote form takes moves "OVER 250 MILES", while the interstate page puts long distance at "about 400 miles", citing a mover (E.E. Ward) [capture]. That list recommends only scores of 7.0 or higher; the small-moves page, which states no threshold, gives Bekins (6.9) a "Best" pick [capture]. v1's score, BBB-grade and markup conflicts were not re-confirmed; the related movers' scores matched across both pages [v1 §2.1; capture].
- **Reviews.** "Write a Review" sits beside "Get a Quote" in the header, but the review form and its rules were not captured [capture]. Third-party feedback feeds an editorial 1-to-10 CMR Score, and no Review or AggregateRating type appears among top-level JSON-LD [capture /editorial-methodology/, /how-we-rank-movers/].

## Disclosure and consent
- **Role and money.** "We're an editorial review site, not a mover, and no mover pays for placement." sits under the fourth H2, below the form [capture /request-a-quote/]. /advertiser-disclosure/ (updated October 26, 2026) says "There are no paid placements on CMR.", while its meta description cites "advertiser compensation" and "how paid placements affect where offers appear" [capture]. Referral fees are hypothetical ("If we ever earn a referral fee…"), where v1 recorded "may earn a referral fee" [capture /advertiser-disclosure/; v1 §2.1].
- **Consent.** "By submitting, you agree to be contacted by a vetted moving specialist. We never sell your info." sits under the step-1 button [capture /request-a-quote/]. Step 2 showed no checkbox and no call or text wording, and no arbitration wording was seen [walk]. The line lacks the written-consent elements listed in compliance facts §3. Whether they were required depends on how the specialist calls, which was not observed [inference].
- **Sharing.** The trust row says "We never sell your info" and "Private by default", but the Privacy Policy was not captured, so who receives the details is unknown [capture /request-a-quote/, /advertiser-disclosure/].
- **Broker-like wording.** Step 2's "accurate quote" line [walk], "booked through the CMR network" and "2,046 BOOKED MOVES PRICED" describe pricing and booking, which sits poorly with "editorial review site" [capture /best-movers-for-small-long-distance-moves/, /]. Matching is framed as CMR's judgment: "we'll match you to the top-rated carriers for your route" [capture /request-a-quote/]. No USDOT or MC number for CMR or its owner was captured [capture]. Compliance facts §2 treats pricing, booking and discretionary assignment as broker risks. This is a risk pattern, not a finding that CMR is a broker.
- **Tracking.** Google Tag Manager, Microsoft Clarity (session replay), Bing UET, Facebook, Google Ads and a script from bzrcdn.openai.com load; no consent-certificate script was detected [capture].

## SEO and content
- **Traffic.** Semrush estimates 191 organic visits and 879 keywords a month, $187 in value and no paid keywords [semrush domain_rank]. That is about a tenth of the next-smallest reference's traffic (budgetvanlines.com, 1,934), though CMR ranks for more keywords (879 vs 178) [semrush; keyword-research §1].
- **Profiles.** /companies/jason-jasons-relocations/ brings 162 visits (84.81%) from 2 keywords, and profiles take 177 of 191 [semrush top_pages_more]. The keywords were not pulled; they are most likely the company's name, which would make the intent navigational [inference].
- **Routes.** Four ZIP-to-ZIP lane URLs earn 1 to 3 visits each, 3.65% combined [semrush top_pages_more]. /routes/state/ca/ has generic H2s and title-cased anchors such as "Fresno Ca 93722 To Inman Sc 29349", while /routes/ promises "real distance, delivery-window, and cost data for that exact move" [capture]. No lane page was captured; the oddly specific ZIP pairs suggest lanes built from single moves [inference].
- **Best-of pages.** The home menu lists at least 10 best-of pages, among them Interstate, Long Distance, Nationwide and Cross-Country, confirming v1's overlap concern [capture /; v1 §2.1]. The two captured lists run 9,708 and 12,344 words; neither is in the top 15, a list that reaches 0-visit rows [capture; semrush top_pages_more].
- **Citable shapes.** The interstate page opens with a quick answer, a jump index and a plain definition ("Interstate means your move crosses a state line") [capture /best-interstate-moving-companies/]. Its related-company disclosure is a separate block, so a quote of the picks can drop it [capture; inference].
- **Architecture and markup.** Best-of lists, route hubs, a /companies/ directory, shipping pages, tools and a six-link trust group hang off a grouped footer [capture; semrush top_pages_more]. FAQPage sits on 4 of 10 pages, Article marks up hubs, and Organization appears only on the home page; only top-level types were recorded [capture].
- **Other.** An "of the month" menu label clashes with "re-checked every 3 to 6 months" [capture /]. URLs include a 2-letter state hub, a "-3" slug suffix and a profile for HireAHelper, a booking marketplace [capture /routes/state/ca/; semrush; v1 §2.4]. Two car-shipping URLs earn 1 and 0 visits, and a "2026 Moving Cost Report" is promoted, but no report URL makes the top 15 [semrush; capture /].
- **For AMC.** CMR is a model for trust patterns, not for SEO. Expect near-zero organic traffic at launch and start with low-difficulty families such as "usdot number lookup" (KD 18) and state routes (KD 10–25) [semrush keyword-research §3; inference].

## What AMC should adopt or adapt
- **Dated licensing rows.** Show USDOT (and MC where held), an FMCSA link and a "verified on" date on every listing and profile, rechecked weekly. Exclude brokers rather than label them, and meet "check a mover I was quoted" with a lookup tool. Serves movers-only and profiles (mover-verification §3, §5; compliance facts §1).
- **Badge with a one-line meaning.** Keep an adjacent explainer and a link to the standard, but use AMC's single "Preferred Mover — American Moving Choice" badge with a clipboard-check glyph and verified-on date: no seal, never "certified". Serves the badge rules (copy draft §8; visual-direction §3, §8; discovery brief §9).
- **Published method, honest gaps.** One versioned method page whose criteria names appear identically everywhere, "data as of" dates, "insufficient data" labels, a data-sources page for FMCSA datasets and refresh cadence, and named authors. Serves trust pages (site-architecture §7.2–§7.3; reference-matrix §3; mover-verification §5).
- **Short first screen and honeypot.** Keep both, but ask ZIP or city, date, size and services first, run the coverage pre-check, then ask for contact details, and describe the steps honestly. Serves the survey order (lead-lifecycle §2).
- **Value before contact.** Put a labeled range and the matched-mover preview after move details and before contact. Pre-fill from the calculator and send its users into the survey, not to a ranking. Serves the value-before-contact goal (site-architecture §6.3; compliance facts §2).
- **Inventory step.** Offer one so movers get cubic feet or weight, framed as preparation for the mover's own survey, since sight-unseen estimates are an FMCSA red flag. Serves lead quality (compliance facts §2; copy draft §6).
- **Answer-first rankings.** Use a quick answer, jump index, mover-check and red-flag sections, and FMCSA-sourced definitions. Put the material-connection line and data date inside the ranking block, link to canonical cost guides instead of repeating them, and use stacked cards on phones. Serves SEO and AI visibility (site-architecture §4.6, §6.1, §7.4; visual-direction §12).
- **Profiles as early traffic.** Prioritize the completeness gate. A profile-started request names that company first at consent while the consumer still picks the count. Serves profiles (site-architecture §4.5; mover-verification §5; copy draft §4) [inference].

## What AMC should avoid
- **Self-interested rankings.** Keep companies with ownership, family or consulting ties to AMC or its staff out of rankings and featured slots, using a related-party check in Admin review, and disclose any tie on the mover's profile. A "scored on its own record" note does not cure the conflict. Badge eligibility is an owner decision; the FTC question is for counsel (mover-verification §4; compliance facts §4).
- **Role and money lines kept on trust pages.** State the role and "How AMC makes money" at survey step 1, consent and confirmation. Generate body, meta and summaries from one text, and never call AMC "independent" (copy draft §1, §7; compliance facts §4).
- **"By submitting" consent.** Use AMC's unticked, named-mover box with automated-call, text, not-a-condition and STOP wording and no arbitration. Keep AMC's own consent record and mask contact fields in any session replay (copy draft §5; compliance facts §3, §8).
- **Absolute no-sale promises.** Movers pay AMC, so flag copy draft §3's "We don't sell your details to anyone else" for counsel with the state-law "sale" question.
- **Broker-like wording.** No claims that AMC quotes, books, prices or picks "the right" mover, no staff sales calls and no sales phone CTA. Describe rule-based eligibility capped by the consumer's count and offer a support contact (compliance facts §2; lead-lifecycle §3).
- **Hidden counts and speed promises.** Ask the count, name eligible movers before submit, repeat the count on confirmation, and promise no timing unless measured (lead-lifecycle §2; visual-direction §13.8).
- **ZIP lanes and parallel lists.** No indexable ZIP pages, state routes only after the gate, one URL per intent, and no shipping verticals before a scope decision (site-architecture §0, §3.1, §4.1).
- **Drifting numbers.** Keep each score, count and threshold in one record rendered into body, meta and JSON-LD, with a test comparing them, and use one "long distance" definition. No unsourced counters, "from" prices tied to members, fake freshness or FAQPage markup (site-architecture §6.3, §7.3, §8.5, §9; lead-lifecycle §3).
- **Open review buttons.** Invite only consumers whose request a mover bought, with evidence labels and ratings kept apart from editorial scores (mover-verification §6).
- **Unchecked "Preferred" naming.** CMR's owner name and Move.org's paid "Preferred Partner" label [capture moveorg.md] blur the word. Run trademark clearance before it enters URLs, titles, badges or schema (site-architecture §1.2, §8.2; counsel).

## Gaps and open questions
- After "Get my matches": matches shown, company count, who calls, how soon and how often.
- Who the "specialist" is, who answers (888) 695-0663, and whether quote leads reach the related movers.
- The full ownership sentence, the privacy policy and the terms.
- Profiles, review pages, the review form, nested JSON-LD, and lane, city and directory pages.
- Estimator output, the AI inventory tool, where the inline forms lead, and what the OpenAI-hosted script does.
- Sources of the counters and the 419 "booked" small moves.
- Mover side: listing path, payments and lead prices.
- Registries for Preferred Movers Network Inc. and "Preferred Movers" trademarks; other firms using the name were reported in search [search, not re-checked].
- BBB, FMCSA complaint and court records, and who holds USDOT 3006268 and 2538365.
- Owner decision: show third-party grades such as BBB on AMC profiles, with source and date, or leave them out (mover-verification §5).
- Semrush lacks CMR's keyword list, backlinks and history; no answer-engine prompts were run.
