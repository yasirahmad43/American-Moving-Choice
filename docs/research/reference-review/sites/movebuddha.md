# moveBuddha: reference review

A moving research and review site that also runs lead paths [capture /moving-cost-calculator/; walk], on the list as a model for a no-contact calculator and data-led city pages [inference].

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-27. **Evidence:** 14 of 14 pages captured; guided walks of the calculator, its results page, the Mover Marketplace first screen and one partner form up to its email step; Semrush US, October 2026; single-run web searches, not re-checked. **Confidence:** medium, because no consent wording, privacy policy or mover-facing page was seen.

## At a glance
- It calls itself "a moving research and review platform" but runs partner forms, a "Mover Marketplace" and a bid path [capture /moving-cost-calculator/; walk /marketplace/].
- Its disclosure says it "may receive compensation" for clicks, webform submissions or calls, naming no payer; no walked path reached consent wording [capture /advertising-disclosure/; walk].
- Its calculator shows prices before any contact field and is its top organic page, at about 4,399 visits a month (11.47%) [walk; semrush top_pages].
- The widget and results page priced one move differently, and other figures drift between pages [walk; capture].
- Partner cards with superlatives and phone numbers recur across templates, unlabeled in the captured text [capture /movers/ri/, /popular-routes/ca/fl/, /blog/binding-vs-non-binding-estimates/].
- About 38,335 US organic visits a month, Authority Score 44 and 9,427 referring domains [semrush domain_rank, backlinks_overview].

- Unclear: on a form headed "Safeway Moving | powered by moveBuddha", "Or call us directly" could mean either firm, and the persona ("Hey! I'm Ryan.") never says whom it works for [walk my.bestinterstatemoving.com/movebuddha/safeway-moving/quote].

## Mover side
No captured page addresses movers, and the footer has no mover sign-up link, so joining, fees, caps and lead checks are unseen [capture]. Partner cards show toll-free numbers, unlike local movers' cards, and Safeway's is also the number its partner form offers [capture /movers/ri/; walk]. [inference] These are probably tracked lines; who answers is unknown.

## Trust, verification and reviews
- **Partner cards.** Safeway, American Van Lines and North American Van Lines appear on every captured city, state and route page under superlatives such as "best overall value", and Safeway tops the LA and Orlando interstate picks [capture /movers/ca/los-angeles/long-distance/, /movers/fl/orlando/long-distance/]. /about-us/ says partner content is labeled, but the text-only capture shows no label on the cards, and affiliate ties "may influence … why they're placed on our site" [capture /about-us/, /advertising-disclosure/]. v1's no-pay-for-ranking claim was not re-confirmed [v1 §2.2].
- **Non-movers and claims.** /movers/ca/ carries PODS and portable-storage cards, and cards make unsourced claims such as "Same-day estimates" [capture /movers/ca/, /movers/fl/orlando/long-distance/].
- **Licensing.** USDOT numbers appear for the three national picks, unchecked against FMCSA here [capture /movers/ca/long-distance/]. Cards say only "licensed insured", though the method claims to confirm "broker vs. carrier status" and guides teach an FMCSA check [capture /blog/binding-vs-non-binding-estimates/, /blog/usdot-lookup/].
- **Badges and protection.** "moveBuddha Certified" and "Our Verified program" overlap, and the evidence shows no recheck cadence or whether movers pay [capture /, /about-us/]. Choosing a Certified mover brings "an extra $1,000 in move coverage", framed as booking "through moveBuddha" [capture /movers/ri/, /]. [inference] That suggests a role beyond passing on leads; it is not a legal conclusion.
- **Scores.** The method publishes weights such as "Moving services (35%)" but reads differently by list type [capture /blog/binding-vs-non-binding-estimates/, /movers/ri/]. Geo star ratings are a composite moveBuddha score presented as analysis, not a customer rating, yet the disclosure calls editorial reviews "personal opinions" [capture /movers/ri/, /movers/ca/los-angeles/long-distance/, /advertising-disclosure/]. [inference] The counts it labels as verified reviews appear to be aggregated third-party reviews. Low scorers stay visible, such as a BBB "F" row [capture /movers/ca/].
- **Scale.** A 400,000 figure counts different things on different pages, and reviews read "1,000,000+" on the home page but "4,000+" in state boilerplate, all unsourced [capture /movers/ri/, /popular-routes/, /, /movers/ca/long-distance/].

## Disclosure and consent
- The "Advertising Disclosure" link opens every page's text and leads the fold, on desktop and mobile, on the 6 pages where fold order was recorded [capture, all 14 pages]. Its 294-word page is hedged and names no payer [capture /advertising-disclosure/]. [inference] Payers are probably featured movers or intermediaries.
- The walk notes show no compensation statement on the results screen or the partner form, which runs on a separately named host, my.bestinterstatemoving.com, whose owner is not shown [walk].
- A footer "Do Not Sell or Share" link appears; the privacy policy and terms were not captured [capture, footer].
- Clarity and Hotjar (which offer session recording), ad tags and an OpenAI pixel host load across the combined 14-page scan, with no consent-certificate script; whether recording was on is unknown [capture, script list].
- Guides embed a "Free Instant Quote" widget while the calculator promises "estimates", and /movers/ca/ offers "Get my best rate →" [capture /blog/usdot-lookup/, /moving-cost-calculator/, /movers/ca/]. "Save up to 30% on your move" and "rates typically drop 15–20%" state no method [walk /moving-cost-calculator-tool/; capture /movers/ca/los-angeles/long-distance/]. v1 recorded the savings claim as 30% or more [v1 §4].

## SEO and content
- **Drivers.** The two calculators bring 5,147 visits (about 13.4%), the largest top-15 family, and the moving cost calculator ranks #4 for its head term (5,400 searches a month, keyword difficulty 49) [semrush top_pages, phrase_these, phrase_organic_top10]. City long-distance pages earn 460–956 visits, /movers/ri/ 222 and two state routes 133–156; 64.5% of traffic sits outside the top 15 [semrush top_pages].
- **Weak fit.** Brand-cost guides, a pay-you-to-move post and the car shipping calculator bring 3,664 visits, more than three city long-distance pages (2,241) [semrush top_pages]. The USDOT guide gets 509 visits against 4,400 searches for "usdot number lookup", a query freight tools led in one search run [semrush top_pages, phrase_these; search: https://www.dat.com/solutions/usdot-number-lookup, third party]. [inference] Freight users may drive much of that demand.
- **URLs and templates.** Geo pages use /movers/{st}/ with 2-letter codes, routes /popular-routes/{st}/{st}/, and many tools /blog/ [capture; semrush top_pages]. LA and Orlando share 10 of 12 heading patterns and RI and CA 12 of 12, with repeated or lightly reworded paragraphs [capture /movers/ca/los-angeles/long-distance/, /movers/fl/orlando/long-distance/, /movers/ri/, /movers/ca/]. v1 also saw a city-to-city route and /movers/tx/buda/long-distance/ [v1 §2.2].
- **Numbers.** California interstate costs read "$613 for a 1-bedroom to $2,622 for a 4-bedroom home" on the LA page but "between $990 and $4,033" on the state long-distance page [capture /movers/ca/los-angeles/long-distance/, /movers/ca/long-distance/]. The route's title and meta ranges differ, though its average cites "847 real-time quotes" over 90 days [capture /popular-routes/ca/fl/].
- **Freshness.** /movers/ca/ shows the capture date as "Last Updated", and the templated LA page claims "a decade" of personal experience [capture /movers/ca/, /movers/ca/los-angeles/long-distance/].
- **Shapes and markup.** Guides open with a quick answer, and the route page adds a "Who regulates interstate moves?" box [capture /blog/usdot-lookup/, /popular-routes/ca/fl/]. One search tool's summaries repeated moveBuddha figures that differ from the live pages (not an AI-engine test) [search: own pages, e.g. https://www.movebuddha.com/popular-routes/ca/fl/ca-to-orlando/]. Geo pages are typed Article, and the calculator has a block that reads as FAQ [capture, page inventory].

## What AMC should adopt or adapt
- **Ungated estimator** [walk; semrush]: live ranges that name missing fields, the full-service range first, container or freight figures labeled as general information, survey prefill, and one versioned model with a consistency test. Serves value before contact (site-architecture §5.3, §6.3).
- **Header "How we make money" link** [capture]: repeat the model in definite words beside quote buttons and at consent. Serves trust pages (consent copy §1, §7).
- **Sourced ranges** [capture /popular-routes/ca/fl/]: sample size, period, source and "data as of" on every range, from member rate cards or cited studies, with title, meta and body from one record. Serves the real-data rule (site-architecture §4.1, §6.3).
- **Details before contact** [walk]: keep "not sure yet" answers and stages; at contact, say that participating movers, up to the number chosen, may receive the details and are named before submit. Serves named-seller consent (consent copy §3–§5).
- **Public method and guides** [capture]: one versioned verification page, a ratings page separating computed numbers from judgment, and FMCSA-sourced check-a-mover guides. Serves trust pages (mover-verification §6, §7).
- **Answer-first geo pages** [capture]: dated answers naming the population, picks computed per market, component columns, low scorers kept and one main call to action. Serves SEO and AI visibility (site-architecture §7.4; visual-direction §12).
- **USDOT on every card** [capture /movers/ca/long-distance/]: add MC, authority type and an FMCSA link; enforce movers-only by FMCSA fields, never names. Serves movers only (mover-verification §1, §3).

## What AMC should avoid
- **Partner cards as picks** [capture]: keep any "Sponsored" slot and the Preferred badge apart from rank and score, keep non-movers out of rankings, and show company claims only with evidence (mover-verification §4, §5; visual-direction §9.4).
- **Drifting numbers** [walk; capture]: one record per figure, an automated title-meta-body check, and defined, dated metrics (site-architecture §6.3, §7.1).
- **Broker-like extras** [capture]: coverage promises, booking or specialist bids; [inference] FMCSA's 2023 guidance weighs involvement in the transaction (compliance facts §2).
- **Quote, best-rate and savings wording** [capture; walk]: say "estimate range" and make no savings claims (compliance facts §2).
- **Murky collection** [walk; capture]: street addresses first, an unclear "us", a separate form domain, or pixels and unmasked recording on survey and consent screens (compliance facts §3).
- **Imported "verified" reviews and stacked badges** [capture]: use AMC's evidence labels and one badge (mover-verification §4, §6).
- **Absolute no-sale lines** [inference]: counsel should test consent copy §3's "We don't sell your details to anyone else" against state privacy definitions.
- **Template filler** [capture; v1]: swap-test failures, small-place and city-pair pages, auto-bumped dates and FAQPage markup (site-architecture §4.1, §7.3, §8.5).
