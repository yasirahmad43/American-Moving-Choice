# moveBuddha: reference review

A moving research and review site that pairs a no-contact cost calculator with partner quote forms, a "Mover Marketplace" and a specialist-call bid path [capture /moving-cost-calculator/; walk]. It is on the list as the best model for an ungated estimator and data-led geo pages, and as a warning about partner cards inside rankings [inference].

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-27. **Evidence:** 14 of 14 pages captured; guided walks of the calculator, its results page, the marketplace's first screen and one partner form up to its email step; Semrush US, October 2026; single-run WebSearch, not re-run by the verifier, so search-only ownership, legal and competitor-program claims are left out. **Confidence:** medium, because no consent wording, privacy policy, mover-facing page or post-email screen was seen.

## At a glance
- It calls itself "a moving research and review platform" yet runs lead paths, and says it "may receive compensation" for clicks, webform submissions or calls [capture /moving-cost-calculator/, /advertising-disclosure/; walk].
- The calculator shows price ranges before any contact field and is the top organic page, at about 4,399 visits a month (11.47%) [walk; semrush top_pages].
- The widget and its results page priced one move differently (freight's top: $7,814 vs $1,761), and other figures drift between pages [walk; capture].
- Partner cards with superlatives, ratings and phone numbers recur on geo and route pages, with no "sponsored" label in the captured text [capture].
- No path reached consent wording; the partner form stops at email after nine move-detail screens [walk].
- About 38,335 US organic visits a month, Authority Score 44 and 9,427 referring domains [semrush domain_rank, backlinks_overview].

## Mover side
No captured page addresses movers: no join path, fees, sharing caps, exclusivity, lead checks or footer sign-up link appeared [capture]. Partner cards show toll-free numbers and local movers' cards local ones, and Safeway's number doubles as the form's "call us" line [capture /movers/ri/; walk]. [inference] These are probably tracked lines. AMC should publish lead prices, caps and the credit policy (site-architecture §1.2; shared-pricing-and-credits) and settle its phone-number policy before profiles launch (site-architecture §0 decision 4).

## Trust, verification and reviews
- **Partner cards.** The evidence pack records Safeway Moving ("best overall value", 4.9/5), American Van Lines and North American Van Lines on every captured city, state and route page; partner ratings run 4.2 to 4.9 [capture]. /about-us/ says partner content is labeled, but the captured card text shows no label [capture]. Affiliate ties "may influence … why they're placed on our site" [capture /advertising-disclosure/].
- **Non-movers and claims.** /movers/ca/ carries a PODS card with a "10% discount", and cards claim "Same-day estimates" without a source [capture /movers/ca/, /movers/fl/orlando/long-distance/].
- **Licensing.** USDOT numbers appear in body text for three national picks [capture /movers/ca/long-distance/]. Cards say only "licensed insured", though the method claims to confirm "broker vs. carrier status" [capture /blog/binding-vs-non-binding-estimates/]. This review did not check the numbers against FMCSA.
- **Badges and protection.** "moveBuddha Certified" and "Our Verified program" overlap, and the evidence does not show whether they are one program or paid [capture /, /about-us/]. Certified movers come with "an extra $1,000 in move coverage", booked "through moveBuddha" [capture /movers/ri/, /]. [inference] That suggests a role beyond passing on leads; it is not a legal conclusion.
- **Scores.** Weights such as "Moving services (35%)" are published, but the method differs by list type [capture /blog/binding-vs-non-binding-estimates/, /movers/ri/]. Geo stars are a composite that includes "moveBuddha's own score" [capture /movers/ri/, /movers/ca/]. The disclosure calls editorial reviews "personal opinions", while geo pages cite "analysis" [capture /advertising-disclosure/, /movers/ca/los-angeles/long-distance/]. [inference] "Verified reviews" appear to be aggregated third-party reviews. Low scorers, such as a BBB "F" row, stay visible [capture /movers/ca/].
- **Scale.** "400,000+" means trusting people, connected people and tool users on different pages, unsourced [capture /movers/ca/los-angeles/long-distance/, /movers/ri/, /popular-routes/]. Reviews are "1,000,000+" on the home page and "4,000+" in state boilerplate [capture /, /movers/ca/long-distance/].

## Disclosure and consent
- The "Advertising Disclosure" link opens the page text on all 14 pages and leads the fold on the 6 where fold order was recorded [capture]. The 294-word page names no payer or pricing model [capture /advertising-disclosure/]. [inference] Payers are probably featured movers or intermediaries.
- The walk notes record no compensation statement on the results screen or in the partner form [walk].
- A footer "Do Not Sell or Share" link is the standard state-privacy opt-out; the privacy policy and terms were not captured [capture].
- The form host, my.bestinterstatemoving.com, is separately named, and its owner is not shown [walk].
- Clarity and Hotjar (which offer session recording), ad pixels and an OpenAI pixel host load across the pooled 14-page scan, with no consent-certificate script [capture]. Whether recording was on is unknown.
- Guides embed a "Free Instant Quote" widget while the calculator calls its output an estimate, and /movers/ca/ offers "Get my best rate →" [capture /blog/usdot-lookup/, /moving-cost-calculator/, /movers/ca/]. "Save up to 30% on your move" and a seasonal "15–20%" price drop state no method [walk; capture /movers/ca/los-angeles/long-distance/].

## SEO and content
- **Drivers.** The two calculators bring 5,147 visits (13.42%), and the site ranks #4 for "moving cost calculator" (5,400 searches a month, keyword difficulty 49) [semrush top_pages, phrase_these, phrase_organic_top10]. City long-distance pages earn 460–956 visits, /movers/ri/ 222 and two state routes 133–156; 64.5% of traffic sits outside the top 15 pages [semrush top_pages].
- **Weak fit.** Brand-cost, countries-that-pay and car-shipping pages bring 3,664 visits, more than the three city long-distance pages (2,241) [semrush top_pages]. The USDOT guide gets 509 visits against 4,400 monthly searches for "usdot number lookup", a query that freight tools led in one search tool [semrush; search: dat.com, third party]. [inference] Freight users may drive much of that demand.
- **URLs.** Geo pages nest under /movers/{st}/ with 2-letter codes, routes under /popular-routes/{st}/{st}/ and many tools under /blog/ [capture; semrush]. v1 also saw numeric-ID profiles, a city-to-city route and /movers/tx/buda/long-distance/ [v1 §2.2].
- **Templates.** LA and Orlando share 10 of 12 heading patterns, RI and CA 12 of 12, with repeated or lightly reworded paragraphs; only the data differs [capture].
- **Numbers.** California interstate costs read "$613 for a 1-bedroom to $2,622" on the LA page but "between $990 and $4,033" on /movers/ca/long-distance/ [capture]. The route title and meta give different ranges, though the page sources its average to "847 real-time quotes" over 90 days [capture /popular-routes/ca/fl/].
- **Freshness.** /movers/ca/ shows the capture date as "Last Updated", the templated LA page claims first-person expertise, and "5 min read" covers 400- and 9,312-word pages [capture].
- **Citable shapes.** Guides open with a quick answer, teach an FMCSA check and compare USDOT and MC numbers; the route page adds a "Who regulates interstate moves?" box [capture /blog/usdot-lookup/, /popular-routes/ca/fl/]. One search tool's summaries quoted moveBuddha figures that differ from the live pages; these are not observed AI-engine citations [search: movebuddha.com's own pages].
- **Markup and titles.** Geo pages use Article, the calculator has a block that reads as FAQ, complete type lists show no aggregateRating, and six of 14 titles add a bracketed promise [capture].

## What AMC should adopt or adapt
- **Ungated estimator.** Lead with the full-service range, label container or freight figures as general information, prefill the survey, and run every range from one versioned model with a consistency test. Serves: value before contact (site-architecture §5.3, §6.3).
- **"How we make money" header link.** Repeat the model beside quote buttons and at consent, in definite words. Serves: trust pages (consent copy §1, §7).
- **Sourced ranges.** Show sample size, period, source and "data as of"; take prices from member rate cards or cited studies; omit thin blocks. Serves: real-data rule (site-architecture §4.1, §6.3).
- **Details before contact.** Keep "not sure yet" answers and stages; at contact, say that up to the chosen number of participating movers may receive the details and are named before submit. Serves: quote survey (consent copy §3–§5).
- **Public method and mover-check guides.** One versioned verification page, a ratings page that separates computed numbers from judgment, and FMCSA-sourced check-a-mover and rights guides. Serves: trust pages (mover-verification §6, §7).
- **Answer-first geo pages.** Date and population in the answer sentence, component columns, low scorers kept, and AMC's mobile order (H1, lede, survey step 1). Serves: SEO and AI visibility (site-architecture §7.4, §7.7; visual-direction §12).
- **USDOT on every card.** Add MC, the authority type and an FMCSA link; enforce movers-only by FMCSA fields, never names. Serves: movers only (mover-verification §1, §3; compliance facts §1).

## What AMC should avoid
- **Partner cards as picks.** Keep the Preferred badge and any "Sponsored" slot apart from rank and score, and keep non-movers out of rankings (visual-direction §9.4; site-architecture §0 decisions 5 and 7).
- **Drifting numbers.** One record feeds title, meta, body and tools; CI fails mismatches; every metric is defined, sourced and dated (site-architecture §6.3).
- **Broker-like extras.** No coverage promises, booking or specialist bids; [inference] FMCSA's 2023 guidance weighs involvement in the transaction (compliance facts §2).
- **Quote and savings wording.** Say "estimate range", name price drivers, note that a written estimate needs the mover's survey, and make no savings claims (compliance facts §2).
- **Murky collection.** No street addresses first, unclear "us", persona without an employer or separate form domain, and no unmasked recording or ad pixels on survey and consent screens (site-architecture §7.1; compliance facts §3).
- **Imported "verified" reviews and stacked badges.** Use AMC's evidence labels and one badge (mover-verification §4, §6).
- **Absolute no-sale lines.** Counsel should test consent copy §3's "We don't sell your details to anyone else" against state "sale" definitions.
- **Template filler.** Swap-test failures, city pairs, small-place clones, fake freshness and FAQPage markup (site-architecture §4.1, §7.3, §8.5).

## Gaps and open questions
- Consent wording, the partner form after email, the marketplace after screen 1 and the exit-intent box.
- How many movers a bid or marketplace request reaches, who sets that number, and who employs the specialist.
- The privacy policy, the terms and the operator of my.bestinterstatemoving.com.
- Mover side: join path, what is paid, pricing model, caps and refunds.
- The FMCSA authority behind USDOT 3166589 and other partners, the $1,000 coverage terms and any visual "sponsored" marker.
- No profile, local city, city-pair or thin-coverage page was captured; JSON-LD lists are clipped; Semrush covers the top 15 pages only; AI engines were not tested.
- Ownership, legal entity, BBB status and legal record were not re-checked.
