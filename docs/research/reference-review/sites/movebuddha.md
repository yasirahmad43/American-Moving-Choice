# moveBuddha: reference review

A moving research and review site that also runs lead paths [capture /moving-cost-calculator/; walk]; one of the owner's 11 references, chiefly for its no-contact calculator and data-led city pages [inference].

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-27. **Evidence:** 14 of 14 pages captured; guided walks of the calculator (prices with no contact details), its results page, the "Mover Marketplace" first screen and one partner form up to its email step; Semrush US, October 2026; single-run WebSearch, not re-checked, so search-only ownership, legal and competitor-program claims are left out. **Confidence:** medium: pages and pre-contact flows are well captured, but no consent wording, privacy policy, terms or mover-facing page was seen.

## At a glance
- It calls itself "a moving research and review platform" yet runs partner forms, a "Mover Marketplace" and a specialist-call bid path [capture /moving-cost-calculator/; walk].
- It "may receive compensation" for clicks, webform submissions or calls, and names no payer or pricing model [capture /advertising-disclosure/].
- The calculator shows three price ranges before any contact field and is the top organic page, at about 4,399 visits a month (11.47%) [walk; semrush top_pages].
- The widget and its results page priced one move differently, and other figures drift between pages [walk; capture].
- Partner cards with superlatives, ratings and phone numbers recur on geo, route and guide pages, with no label in the captured text [capture].
- No walked path reached consent wording [walk].
- About 38,335 US organic visits a month, Authority Score 44 and 9,427 referring domains [semrush domain_rank, backlinks_overview].

- Unclear: "Or call us directly at (855) 650-0657" does not say whether "us" is Safeway or moveBuddha, and the persona ("Hey! I'm Ryan.") never says whom it works for [walk, partner form].

- Inconsistent: for Atlanta 30303 to Miami 33101 (2-bedroom apartment), freight topped out at $7,814 in the widget but $1,761 on the results page, which calls its ranges "a rough estimate" [walk /moving-cost-calculator/, /moving-cost-calculator-tool/].

[inference] These are probably tracked lines; who answers is unknown.

## Trust, verification and reviews
- **Partner cards.** Safeway Moving ("best overall value"), American Van Lines and North American Van Lines appear on every captured city, state and route page, and Safeway leads the interstate picks on the LA and Orlando pages; card ratings run 4.2 to 4.9 [capture /movers/ca/los-angeles/long-distance/, /movers/fl/orlando/long-distance/]. /about-us/ says partner content is labeled, yet the captured card text shows no label (the capture is text only) [capture]. Affiliate ties "may influence … why they're placed on our site" [capture /advertising-disclosure/]. v1's record that no mover pays for ranking was not re-confirmed [v1 §2.2].
- **Non-movers and claims.** /movers/ca/ carries PODS and portable-storage cards, one with a "10% discount", and a card claims "Same-day estimates" without a source [capture /movers/ca/, /movers/fl/orlando/long-distance/].
- **Licensing.** USDOT numbers appear in body text for the three national picks, unchecked against FMCSA here [capture /movers/ca/long-distance/]. Cards say only "licensed insured", though the method claims to confirm "broker vs. carrier status" [capture /blog/binding-vs-non-binding-estimates/].
- **Badges and protection.** "moveBuddha Certified" and "Our Verified program" overlap, with no stated recheck cadence or fee [capture /, /about-us/]. Certified movers add "an extra $1,000 in move coverage", booked "through moveBuddha", and the results page pairs added protection with the specialist call [capture /movers/ri/, /; walk]. [inference] That suggests a role beyond passing on leads; it is not a legal conclusion.
- **Scores.** Weights such as "Moving services (35%)" are published, but the method appears in several places and differs by list type [capture /blog/binding-vs-non-binding-estimates/, /movers/ri/]. Geo stars are a composite that includes "moveBuddha's own score", while the disclosure calls its editorial reviews "personal opinions" [capture /movers/ca/, /advertising-disclosure/]. [inference] The "verified reviews" counts appear to be aggregated third-party reviews. Source-labeled Yelp and BBB ratings sit beside scores, and low scorers stay visible, such as a BBB "F" row [capture /movers/ri/, /movers/ca/].
- **Guidance.** Guides teach an FMCSA check, the route page lists the complaint line, and state pages explain consumer rights and released-value coverage [capture /blog/usdot-lookup/, /popular-routes/ca/fl/, /movers/ri/].
- **Scale.** The 400,000 figure means people who trust, people connected or tool users on three pages, unsourced, and reviews read "1,000,000+" on the home page but "4,000+" in state boilerplate [capture /movers/ri/, /popular-routes/, /, /movers/ca/long-distance/]. Visits run about 460,000 a year, but visits are not people [semrush keyword-research §1].

## Disclosure and consent
- The "Advertising Disclosure" link opens every page's text and leads the fold, on desktop and mobile, on the 6 pages where fold order was recorded [capture]. Its 294-word page is hedged and names no payer [capture /advertising-disclosure/]. [inference] Payers are probably featured movers or intermediaries.
- The walk notes show no compensation statement on the results screen or partner form [walk].
- The footer has a "Do Not Sell or Share" link, but the privacy policy and terms were not captured [capture].
- The partner form runs on a separately named host, my.bestinterstatemoving.com, whose owner is not shown [walk].
- Clarity and Hotjar (which offer session recording), ad tags from Facebook, Bing, Google and Reddit, and an OpenAI pixel host load across the pooled 14-page scan, with no consent-certificate script; whether recording was on is unknown [capture].
- A "Free Instant Quote" widget sits on guides while the calculator calls its output an estimate, and /movers/ca/ offers "Get my best rate →" [capture /blog/usdot-lookup/, /moving-cost-calculator/, /movers/ca/]. "Save up to 30% on your move" and "rates typically drop 15–20%" state no method; v1 had recorded 30% or more [walk /moving-cost-calculator-tool/; capture /movers/ca/los-angeles/long-distance/; v1 §4].

## SEO and content
- **Drivers.** The two calculators bring 5,147 visits (about 13.4%), the largest family in the top 15, and the site ranks #4 for "moving cost calculator" (5,400 searches a month, keyword difficulty 49) [semrush top_pages, phrase_these, phrase_organic_top10]. City long-distance pages earn 460–956 visits, /movers/ri/ 222 and two state routes 133–156, and about 64.5% of traffic sits outside the top 15 [semrush top_pages].
- **Weak fit.** Brand-cost, countries-that-pay and car-shipping pages bring 3,664 visits, more than three city long-distance pages (2,241) [semrush top_pages]. The USDOT guide gets 509 visits against 4,400 monthly searches for "usdot number lookup", a query freight tools led in one search run [semrush top_pages, phrase_these; search: https://www.dat.com/solutions/usdot-number-lookup, third party]. [inference] Freight users may drive much of that demand.
- **URLs.** Geo pages use /movers/{st}/ with 2-letter codes, routes /popular-routes/{st}/{st}/, and many tools sit under /blog/ [capture; semrush top_pages]. v1 also saw numeric-ID profiles, a city-to-city route and /movers/tx/buda/long-distance/ [v1 §2.2]. [inference] That hints at pages built from place lists.
- **Templates.** LA and Orlando share 10 of 12 heading patterns and RI and CA 12 of 12, with repeated or lightly reworded paragraphs [capture]. Link modules point to nearby cities and routes, and the footer lists hubs, not cities [capture /movers/ri/].
- **Numbers.** California interstate costs read "$613 for a 1-bedroom to $2,622 for a 4-bedroom home" on the LA page but "between $990 and $4,033" on /movers/ca/long-distance/ [capture]. The route's title and meta give different ranges, though the page sources its average to "847 real-time quotes" over 90 days [capture /popular-routes/ca/fl/]. The calculator says its data updates "continuously" and "monthly", partly from "anonymized user quotes from this very tool" [capture /moving-cost-calculator/].
- **Freshness.** /movers/ca/ shows the capture date as "Last Updated", the templated LA page claims "a decade" of personal experience, and "5 min read" covers 400- and 9,312-word pages [capture /movers/ca/, /movers/ca/los-angeles/long-distance/, /popular-routes/].
- **Shapes and markup.** Guides open with a quick answer and compare USDOT and MC numbers, and the route page has a "Who regulates interstate moves?" box [capture /blog/usdot-lookup/, /popular-routes/ca/fl/]. One search tool's summaries quoted moveBuddha figures that differ from the live pages; these are not AI-engine citations [search: own pages, e.g. https://www.movebuddha.com/popular-routes/ca/fl/ca-to-orlando/]. Geo pages are typed Article, the calculator has a block that reads as FAQ, and six of 14 titles add a bracketed promise such as a year or price [capture].

## What AMC should adopt or adapt
- **Ungated estimator** [walk; semrush]: live ranges, a missing-fields line and AMC's own no-contact promise; full-service range first, container or freight figures labeled as general information; inputs that prefill the survey; one versioned model with a consistency test. Serves value before contact (site-architecture §5.3, §6.3).
- **Header "How we make money" link** [capture]: repeat the model in definite words beside quote buttons and at consent, with one role statement everywhere. Serves trust pages (consent copy §1, §7; site-architecture §7.1).
- **Sourced ranges** [capture /popular-routes/ca/fl/]: sample size, period, source and "data as of" on every range; prices from member rate cards or cited studies; title, meta and body from one record; no block below the gate's data minimum. Serves the real-data rule (site-architecture §4.1 G4, §6.3).
- **Details before contact** [walk]: keep "not sure yet" answers and stages; at contact, say that participating movers, up to the number the consumer picks, may receive the details and are named before submit. Serves the survey and named-seller consent (consent copy §3–§5).
- **Public method and guides** [capture]: one versioned verification page, a ratings page separating computed numbers from judgment, and FMCSA-sourced check-a-mover, rights and valuation guides. Serves trust pages (mover-verification §6, §7).
- **Answer-first geo pages** [capture]: date and population in the answer sentence, picks computed per market, component columns, low scorers kept, one primary call to action and AMC's mobile order. Serves SEO and AI visibility (site-architecture §7.4, §7.7; visual-direction §12).
- **USDOT on every card** [capture /movers/ca/long-distance/]: add MC, authority type and an FMCSA link; enforce movers-only by FMCSA authority fields, never names. Serves movers only (mover-verification §1, §3; compliance facts §1).

## What AMC should avoid
- **Partner cards as picks** [capture]: keep the Preferred badge and any "Sponsored" slot apart from rank and score, keep container and DIY brands out of mover rankings, and show company claims only with evidence (mover-verification §4, §5; visual-direction §9.4).
- **Drifting numbers** [walk; capture]: one record per figure, an automated title-meta-body check, every metric defined and dated, and no visitor count presented as people connected (site-architecture §6.3, §7.1).
- **Broker-like extras** [capture; walk]: no coverage promises, booking or specialist bids; [inference] FMCSA's 2023 guidance weighs involvement in the transaction (compliance facts §2).
- **Quote, best-rate and savings wording** [capture; walk]: say "estimate range", name price drivers and make no savings claims (compliance facts §2).
- **Murky collection** [walk; capture]: street addresses first, an unclear "us", a separate form domain, or pixels and unmasked recording on survey and consent screens (compliance facts §3).
- **Imported "verified" reviews and stacked badges** [capture]: use AMC's evidence labels and one badge (mover-verification §4, §6).
- **Absolute no-sale lines** [inference]: counsel should test consent copy §3's "We don't sell your details to anyone else" against state privacy definitions.
- **Template filler** [capture; v1]: swap-test failures, small-place and city-pair pages, auto-bumped dates and FAQPage markup (site-architecture §4.1, §7.3, §8.5).
