# moveBuddha: reference review

A moving research and review site that also runs lead paths [capture /moving-cost-calculator/; walk]; among the owner's 11 references it matters most for its no-contact calculator and data-led city pages [inference].

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-27. **Evidence:** 14 of 14 pages captured; guided walks of the calculator, its results page, the "Mover Marketplace" first screen and one partner form up to its email step; Semrush US, October 2026; single-run WebSearch, not re-checked, so search-only ownership and legal claims are left out. **Confidence:** medium, because no consent wording, privacy policy or mover-facing page was seen.

## At a glance
- It calls itself "a moving research and review platform" but runs partner forms, a "Mover Marketplace" and a bid path [capture /moving-cost-calculator/; walk].
- It says it "may receive compensation" for clicks, webform submissions or calls, without naming the payer or pricing model [capture /advertising-disclosure/].
- Its calculator shows price ranges before any contact field and is its top organic page, at about 4,399 visits a month (11.47%) [walk; semrush top_pages].
- The widget and results page priced one move differently, and other figures drift between pages [walk; capture].
- Partner cards with superlatives, ratings and phone numbers recur across templates, unlabeled in the captured text [capture].
- No walked path reached consent wording [walk].
- About 38,335 US organic visits a month, Authority Score 44 and 9,427 referring domains [semrush domain_rank, backlinks_overview].

## Trust, verification and reviews
- **Partner cards.** Safeway, American Van Lines and North American Van Lines appear on every captured city, state and route page with superlatives such as "best overall value", and Safeway leads the LA and Orlando interstate picks [capture /movers/ca/los-angeles/long-distance/, /movers/fl/orlando/long-distance/]. /about-us/ says partner content is labeled, but the captured card text shows no label (text-only capture), and affiliate ties "may influence … why they're placed on our site" [capture /about-us/, /advertising-disclosure/]. v1's no-pay-for-ranking claim was not re-confirmed [v1 §2.2].
- **Non-movers and claims.** /movers/ca/ carries PODS and portable-storage cards, one with a "10% discount", and cards make unsourced claims such as "Same-day estimates" [capture /movers/ca/, /movers/fl/orlando/long-distance/].
- **Licensing.** USDOT numbers appear for the three national picks, unchecked against FMCSA here [capture /movers/ca/long-distance/]. Cards say only "licensed insured", though the method claims to confirm "broker vs. carrier status" [capture /blog/binding-vs-non-binding-estimates/].
- **Badges and protection.** "moveBuddha Certified" and "Our Verified program" overlap, with no stated recheck cadence or fee [capture /, /about-us/]. Certified movers add "an extra $1,000 in move coverage", booked "through moveBuddha" [capture /movers/ri/, /]. [inference] That suggests a role beyond passing on leads; it is not a legal conclusion.
- **Scores.** The method publishes weights such as "Moving services (35%)" but reads differently by list type [capture /blog/binding-vs-non-binding-estimates/, /movers/ri/]. Geo stars are a composite including "moveBuddha's own score", yet the disclosure calls editorial reviews "personal opinions" [capture /movers/ca/, /advertising-disclosure/]. [inference] "Verified reviews" counts appear to be aggregated third-party reviews. Low scorers stay visible, such as a BBB "F" row [capture /movers/ca/].
- **Guidance.** Guides teach an FMCSA check, and state pages explain consumer rights and released-value coverage [capture /blog/usdot-lookup/, /movers/ri/].
- **Scale.** The 400,000 figure means trusting people, connected people or tool users on different pages, unsourced, and reviews read "1,000,000+" on the home page but "4,000+" in state boilerplate [capture /movers/ri/, /popular-routes/, /, /movers/ca/long-distance/]. Semrush visits, about 460,000 a year, are not people, so these can't be tested [semrush keyword-research §1].

## Disclosure and consent
- The "Advertising Disclosure" link opens every page's text and leads the fold on the 6 pages where fold order was recorded [capture]. Its 294-word page is hedged and names no payer [capture /advertising-disclosure/]. [inference] Payers are probably featured movers or intermediaries.
- The walk notes show no compensation statement on the results screen or partner form [walk].
- A footer "Do Not Sell or Share" link appears, but the privacy policy and terms were not captured [capture].
- The partner form runs on a separately named host, my.bestinterstatemoving.com, whose owner is not shown [walk].
- Clarity and Hotjar (which offer session recording), ad tags and an OpenAI pixel host load across the pooled scan, with no consent-certificate script [capture].
- Guides embed a "Free Instant Quote" widget while the calculator promises "estimates", and /movers/ca/ offers "Get my best rate →" [capture /blog/usdot-lookup/, /moving-cost-calculator/, /movers/ca/]. "Save up to 30% on your move" and "rates typically drop 15–20%" state no method [walk /moving-cost-calculator-tool/; capture /movers/ca/los-angeles/long-distance/]; v1 had recorded 30% or more [v1 §4].

## SEO and content
- **Drivers.** The two calculators bring 5,147 visits (about 13.4%), the largest top-15 family, and the site ranks #4 for "moving cost calculator" (5,400 searches a month, keyword difficulty 49) [semrush top_pages, phrase_these, phrase_organic_top10]. City long-distance pages earn 460–956 visits, /movers/ri/ 222 and two state routes 133–156, and 64.5% of traffic sits outside the top 15 [semrush top_pages].
- **Weak fit.** Brand-cost, countries-that-pay and car-shipping pages bring 3,664 visits, more than three city long-distance pages (2,241) [semrush top_pages]. The USDOT guide gets 509 visits against 4,400 searches for "usdot number lookup", which freight tools led in one search run [semrush top_pages, phrase_these; search: https://www.dat.com/solutions/usdot-number-lookup, third party]. [inference] Freight users may drive much of that demand.
- **URLs.** Geo pages use /movers/{st}/ with 2-letter codes, routes /popular-routes/{st}/{st}/, and many tools /blog/ [capture; semrush top_pages]. v1 also saw numeric-ID profiles, a city-to-city route and /movers/tx/buda/long-distance/ [v1 §2.2].
- **Templates.** LA and Orlando share 10 of 12 heading patterns and RI and CA 12 of 12, with repeated or lightly reworded paragraphs [capture]. Link modules point to nearby cities and routes, and the footer lists hubs, not cities [capture /movers/ri/].
- **Numbers.** California interstate costs read "$613 for a 1-bedroom to $2,622 for a 4-bedroom home" on the LA page but "between $990 and $4,033" on /movers/ca/long-distance/ [capture]. The route's title and meta ranges differ, though its average cites "847 real-time quotes" over 90 days [capture /popular-routes/ca/fl/]. The calculator claims both "continuously" and "monthly" data updates [capture /moving-cost-calculator/].
- **Freshness.** /movers/ca/ shows the capture date as "Last Updated", the templated LA page claims "a decade" of personal experience, and "5 min read" covers 400- and 9,312-word pages [capture /movers/ca/, /movers/ca/los-angeles/long-distance/, /popular-routes/].
- **Shapes and markup.** Guides open with a quick answer, and the route page adds a "Who regulates interstate moves?" box [capture /blog/usdot-lookup/, /popular-routes/ca/fl/]. One search tool's summaries quoted moveBuddha figures that differ from the live pages; that is not an AI-engine test [search: own pages, e.g. https://www.movebuddha.com/popular-routes/ca/fl/ca-to-orlando/]. Geo pages are typed Article, the calculator has a block that reads as FAQ, and six of 14 titles add a bracketed promise [capture].

## What AMC should adopt or adapt
- **Ungated estimator** [walk; semrush]: show live ranges and name missing fields; lead with the full-service range, label container or freight figures as general information, prefill the survey, and run one versioned model with a consistency test. Serves value before contact (site-architecture §5.3, §6.3).
- **Header "How we make money" link** [capture]: repeat the model in definite words beside quote buttons and at consent. Serves trust pages (consent copy §1, §7).
- **Sourced ranges** [capture /popular-routes/ca/fl/]: sample size, period, source and "data as of" on every range, from member rate cards or cited studies, with title, meta and body from one record. Serves the real-data rule (site-architecture §4.1 G4, §6.3).
- **Details before contact** [walk]: keep "not sure yet" answers and stages; at contact, say that participating movers, up to the number chosen, may receive the details and are named before submit. Serves the survey and named-seller consent (consent copy §3–§5).
- **Public method and guides** [capture]: one versioned verification page, a ratings page separating computed numbers from judgment, and FMCSA-sourced check-a-mover and rights guides. Serves trust pages (mover-verification §6, §7).
- **Answer-first geo pages** [capture]: dated answer sentences naming the population, picks computed per market, component columns, low scorers kept, one main call to action. Serves SEO and AI visibility (site-architecture §7.4, §7.7; visual-direction §12).
- **USDOT on every card** [capture /movers/ca/long-distance/]: add MC, authority type and an FMCSA link; enforce movers-only by FMCSA fields, never names. Serves movers only (mover-verification §1, §3; compliance facts §1).

## What AMC should avoid
- **Partner cards as picks** [capture]: keep any "Sponsored" slot and the Preferred badge apart from rank and score, keep container and DIY brands out of mover rankings, and show company claims only with evidence (mover-verification §4, §5; visual-direction §9.4).
- **Drifting numbers** [walk; capture]: one record per figure, an automated title-meta-body check, defined and dated metrics, and no visitors described as people connected (site-architecture §6.3, §7.1).
- **Broker-like extras** [capture]: coverage promises, booking or specialist bids; [inference] FMCSA's 2023 guidance weighs involvement in the transaction (compliance facts §2).
- **Quote, best-rate and savings wording** [capture; walk]: say "estimate range" and make no savings claims (compliance facts §2).
- **Murky collection** [walk; capture]: street addresses first, an unclear "us", a separate form domain, or pixels and unmasked recording on survey and consent screens (compliance facts §3).
- **Imported "verified" reviews and stacked badges** [capture]: use AMC's evidence labels and one badge (mover-verification §4, §6).
- **Absolute no-sale lines** [inference]: counsel should test consent copy §3's "We don't sell your details to anyone else" against state privacy definitions.
- **Template filler** [capture; v1]: swap-test failures, small-place and city-pair pages, auto-bumped dates, FAQPage markup (site-architecture §4.1, §7.3, §8.5).
